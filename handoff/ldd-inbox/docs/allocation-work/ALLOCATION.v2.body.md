# ALLOCATION: who builds which idea (Level Devil Dad)

Written 2026-11-27 by the uniqueness allocator. Inputs: TROLL-BIBLE.md, 21 city bibles (LEH included), MECHANICS.md, CITIES.md. Working index: allocation-work/index.md. Machine form: allocation.json (full A2/A3 and closest-idea text per slot; this file clips those two columns for size).

**Result.** 36 cities, 417 idea families, each with exactly one owner city and one row below; 0 duplicate (family, betrayal) pairs; 0 betrayal types repeated inside a city; 21 new cities hold 10 stages + 2 reserves; 15 shipped cities get an encore pair (09-10), one new tour betrayal and one new seventh-death theme. Shape per new city: 01 tour, 02-04 standard, 05-06 long, 07 marathon, 08 HWIK ("how would I know"), 09 tough but knowable, 10 finale. At most 2 HWIK per city, never adjacent, never 01, 09 or 10. Where a bible proposed two marathons the second runs as slot 09 with 4-5 beats.

**Caveat.** 'FRESH' concepts and 'unverified' places were added by the allocator where retired ideas left a hole; each needs a source before build.

## 6. How a builder uses this file

1. Find your city in section 2 (new) or section 3 (shipped). Slots 01-10 are your stages in order; R1, R2 are reserves.
2. Build only the concept id in your slot. The family and betrayal columns are your key: nobody else owns it. If a reserve is needed, take R1 then R2; never an idea from another city's table.
3. Before coding, grep the nouns and verbs of your idea (TROLL-BIBLE section G) and read the "almost used" list; if your idea is near one, change the mechanic, not the dressing.
4. Chains: A2 is the obvious fix and dies to a different trap, A3 is one move. Marathon slots carry their chain plan in the table; HWIK slots name their tell and must satisfy TROLL-BIBLE section D. Never add a second HWIK or a betrayal type already in your city.
5. Slot 09 is tough but knowable, slot 10 calls back only your own city. Shipped cities add only the encore pair, the new tour betrayal and the new seventh-death theme.
6. Run `python3 allocation-work/check.py` before pushing (no duplicate family, no duplicate pair, one betrayal type per city).
