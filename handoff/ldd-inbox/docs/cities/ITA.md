# ITA · Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = every word checked in a listed source, U = unverified (with a verified fallback). Nothing here reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities: SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis; **ITA = the first sunrise, early dark and shadow, brick walls, cane bridges, rice-fish paddies, mithun**; KOH = hornbill festival, morung, hill lanes; IMP = floating ground, polo; AIZ = stilt houses, stairs, bamboo poles; AGT = palace water, rubber, rickshaws; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons.

Sources: [W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar) · [W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh) · [W-DONG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong,_Arunachal_Pradesh) · [WV-ITA](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Itanagar) · [UNESCO Ziro](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5893/) · [W-NISHI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nishi_language) · [LH-Nyishi](https://www.languageshome.com/English-Nyishi.htm) · [W-ARL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_languages) · [eSikkim: Itanagar](https://www.esikkimtourism.in/things-to-do-in-itanagar/) · [Sentinel: Dong](https://www.sentinelassam.com/breakingnews/sunrise-festival-20-dong-valley-to-host-new-year-celebrations-from-december-30)

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a young hill capital (founded as the capital in the 1970s, [W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar)) at ~320 m, named for a 14th-15th-century brick fort (ita = brick, [W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar)); the state name means the land of the dawn-lit mountains ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)); 26+ communities share Hindi as the link language ([Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-makes-Nagaland-and-Arunachal-Pradesh-the-most-linguistically-diverse-states-in-India?top_ans=57132324), (unverified source type)).
- Visitors: Ita Fort, Ganga Lake (Gyakar Sinyi), then on to Ziro's paddy valley, Tawang, Sela, Namdapha ([W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar), [eSikkim](https://www.esikkimtourism.in/things-to-do-in-itanagar/)). An Inner Line Permit is needed to enter ([WV-ITA](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Itanagar)).
- Pride and laugh: "we see the sun first" (Dong village gets India's first sunrise, [W-DONG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong,_Arunachal_Pradesh)) and so we also lose it first; the whole state is a hill road with a landslide on it.

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
1. **Ita Fort** · brick ramparts on natural ridges, irregular shape, 14-15th c. ([W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar)) · low red-brown wall with a gate · betrayal: bricks as a loose wall (concept 3).
2. **Ganga Lake (Gyakar Sinyi)** · a forest-ringed lake for picnics ([W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar), [weekendtraveles](https://weekendtraveles.wordpress.com/)) · calm oval pond · seed: picnic mats; water is a taken theme.
3. **Dong valley sunrise** · tourists climb a peak at 3 am for India's first light; Dong sits at 1,240 m ([W-DONG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong,_Arunachal_Pradesh)) · a dark ridge with a thin orange line · betrayal: concept 1.
4. **Ziro valley** · UNESCO tentative list; Apatani farming with rice and fish together ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5893/), [plantrip](https://plantrip.io/itinerary/1041029)) · flat green paddies in bunds with bamboo pipes · concept 4.
5. **Cane and bamboo suspension bridges** (CITIES.md) · a deck of slats hung from cane loops over a river · concept 2. Not the recoil rope of PNJ-05.
6. **Sela Pass and Sela lake** · high pass with snow and a lake (kettle lake at Se La, [W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)) · white pass, blue lake, a stalled truck · concept 9. Use only the pass, never the border.
7. **Tawang** · its monastery is the largest in India (weekendtraveles, (unverified)) · religious: NOT a mechanic; only a distant roofline silhouette.
8. **Namdapha / Pakke / Mouling reserves** · 3 tiger reserves, ~750 bird species, hoolock gibbon, giant flying squirrels ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)) · forest canopy · concept 8 (fig branches).
9. **Siang (Brahmaputra) river** · major river of the state ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)) · wide brown water with a bamboo raft · concept 10.
10. **Itanagar Wildlife Sanctuary** · clouded leopard, Asiatic black bear ([eSikkim](https://www.esikkimtourism.in/things-to-do-in-itanagar/)) · seed: forest only; do not make a predator chaser (taken).
11. **Dambuk oranges** (CITIES.md) · terraced orange groves · concept 7.
12. **Mithun** · state animal of Arunachal (CITIES.md; Kohima's zoo also keeps mithun, [WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · big dark bovine with pale legs and a wide forehead · concept 6.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Overnight super-express buses to Guwahati (~12 h), a state bus service, Naharlagun station 15 km away with a day train and a Shatabdi to Guwahati ([WV-ITA](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Itanagar)); shared Sumos (unverified); hill roads close in landslides (CITIES.md). Trap: a road that lets one direction through at a time (concept 11, "convoy").
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **pika pila**, bamboo-shoot pickle (CITIES.md; unverified spelling) · (2) **thukpa** (also GTK's, so avoid) · (3) **bamboo-shoot** dishes. Recommend **pika pila**: a jar is a clean, friendly shape. Not apong (rice beer). Also rice-fish from Ziro ([plantrip](https://plantrip.io/itinerary/1041029)), momos (unverified).
- **Markets.** Village women sell leafy vegetables roadside (unverified, Quora); Naharlagun and Itanagar vegetable markets. Trap: a stall on the bridge.
- **Weather.** 2,000-5,000 mm rain a year, 70-80% May-October ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)); Itanagar is humid subtropical with mild winters ([W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar)). Sun rises early in the east (Dong).
- **Animals.** Mithun, hornbill (the great hornbill is a forest bird; beak headgear is on the AVOID list, so draw the bird only), hoolock gibbon, giant flying squirrel ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)). No gliding (taken).
- **Crafts.** Cane and bamboo work; weaving (unverified).
- **Sports and games.** Local games (unverified); the capital hosts athletic meets ([WV-ITA](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Itanagar) has a photo). Archery is not used (RNC-03).
- **Festivals (backdrop only).** Nyokum (Nyishi, February), Solung (Adi harvest, September), Losar (Monpa), Ziro Music Festival ([thrillophilia](https://www.thrillophilia.com/ziro-music-festival)); Dong Sunrise festival from Dec 2025 ([W-DONG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong,_Arunachal_Pradesh)). Show decorations; never a hazard or a joke.
- **Habits.** Link language is Hindi, spoken in a simplified, friendly way; people greet with "Namaste"; many use "dada/didi" (unverified).

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the welcome path runs into Ita Fort; the first brick you hop at is mortar, the second is loose.

1. **Teen Baje (3 a.m. dawn climb)** · hook: Dong's 3 a.m. climb ([W-DONG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dong,_Arunachal_Pradesh)) · expect: climb in the dark with a torch · betrayal: the torch only shows floors within a small circle; two floors look alike; one is a decoy that fades when the torch leaves; at dawn the true route lights up as the sun line crosses the ridge and the decoys show red. · second rule: stand still to keep the torch circle large; second betrayal: standing still makes the sun-line reach the ridge and a decoy drop. · door: the viewpoint at the summit, visible only when the sun-line reaches it. · length: "how would I know" · closest: `lamp`, powercut BLR-06, mist pits TVM-07; different: your light radius reveals the real floors and the dawn reveals which was a decoy.
2. **Ek Ek Karke (one at a time)** · hook: cane bridge etiquette (CITIES.md) · expect: cross quickly · betrayal: a porter with a load steps on from the far end; you cannot pass on the deck; passing bays exist every third slat; the deck shakes more with two. · second rule: wait at the first bay; the porter crosses; second betrayal: a second porter arrives early. · door: a hanging cane gate. · length: standard · closest: scooters from behind and head-on TVM-03, passed plank DMN-04; different: one-lane bridge with a walking oncoming load and bays.
3. **Ita (the brick wall)** · hook: Ita Fort ([W-ITA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itanagar)) · expect: a solid wall to climb · betrayal: the wall is built of bricks: a hop at one pops it out (a window or a foothold) and the bricks above shift down; pull the wrong one and the wall slumps; two bricks back is the real stair. · second rule: pull from the top; second betrayal: top bricks hold spikes. · door: the fort gate that appears when the wall is half removed. · length: long · closest: ant bridges RPR-07, dhuska flip RNC-02; different: you remove blocks of a pile and gravity rearranges it.
4. **Dhaan aur Machhli (rice and fish)** · hook: Apatani paddies ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5893/)) · expect: paddies are floors · betrayal: each paddy is a bamboo-channel-fed tank; a lever lowers one and raises the next; you can only stand on a paddy when its water is low and swim a tank when high (air meter taken KVT-06; so tanks are walk-through-water only up to knees). · second rule: lever order from the end. · second betrayal: the last lever resets the first. · door: the sluice at the end. · length: long · closest: tide DMN-05, floods TVM-02; different: connected tanks, water moves from one place to another by your actions.
5. **Pahaad ki Chhaaya (the hill's shadow)** · hook: sun leaves the valley early (Dong sunrise; unverified for dusk) · expect: shade is safe · betrayal: ground in shade is frost (slides), ground in sun is wet (grippy); the shadow edge moves one tile per second from the right. · second rule: stay at the edge; second betrayal: the edge jumps over a gap. · door: the sunny door. · length: standard · closest: melting ice DMN-03, sunbeams GNR-03; different: a moving edge turns floors slippery.
6. **Mithun Maidan (mithun meadow)** · hook: state animal · expect: stand on the cattle · betrayal: grazing heads dip in turn; a dipped head is a step; a calf walks under; the lead cow blocks. · second rule: wait for the lead's lift; second betrayal: the third head dips as you land. · door: a gate the herd opens. · length: standard · closest: gossip bubbles PNJ-02, lions GNR-05; different: living platforms on a graze cycle.
7. **Santra Seedhi (orange terraces)** · hook: Dambuk oranges (CITIES.md) · expect: collect oranges · betrayal: a hop under a branch shakes oranges down a slope that roll into baskets that become steps; fall off and they roll the other way. · second rule: shake from the lower tree; second betrayal: the top tree drops a whole basket on you. · door: a crate door. · length: standard · closest: coconuts TVM-04; different: fruit is steps, not hazard.
8. **Anjeer Daal (fig canopy)** · hook: forest birds and giant squirrels ([W-ARP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh)) · expect: branch walk · betrayal: figs ripen from the edge inward and the ripe ones drop as platforms; unripe ones are rock; a flying squirrel passes under. · second rule: ripen order shown by colour; second betrayal: colour flips at dusk. · door: the hollow. · length: marathon (3 checkpoints) · closest: bending palms KVT-07; different: ripeness timeline.
9. **Sela Dhakka (push the truck)** · hook: Sela pass (cold start) · expect: walk past · betrayal: a stalled truck blocks the road; you push; the truck rolls back when you stop, killing the thing behind; pushing adds speed in steps. · second rule: push in bursts; second betrayal: a second truck on the next hill. · door: the truck's tailgate. · length: marathon · closest: iron and magnets BPL-07; different: pushing a heavy slope-bound object (no push mechanic yet; verify with `grep -rni push src/game`).
10. **Bans Ka Bera (bamboo raft)** · hook: Siang river · expect: ride across · betrayal: the raft's poles drift apart with weight; shift weight to hold them; rapids; the middle pole is a decoy. · door: a bank door. · length: how would I know · closest: pedal boat DMN-02; different: the deck is made of separate poles.
11. **Convoy Gate (hill road)** · hook: hill roads and landslides (CITIES.md) · expect: traffic · betrayal: a one-way gate for up and down convoys; you can only walk in the convoy's direction; a landslide stops one lane. · door: gate arm. · length: standard · closest: signals BLR-04; different: direction alternates by gate.
12. **Subah (the full dawn)** · calls back only Itanagar ideas: torch circle (1), one-lane bridge (2), bricks (3), sluice (4), shadow (5). · door: the sunrise door at the end; only the sun-line reveals. · length: finale.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky pre-dawn indigo to orange **#1F2A4A / #F2A65A**; hills deep forest **#2E5A3C**; tiles brick red **#9B4A32**; accent orange **#F28C28** (unverified, from descriptions).
- Skyline: bamboo-and-timber hill houses on stilts (unverified), brick fort wall silhouette, a lake edge, a long ridge.
- Ground cap: moss over red brick; wet clay.
- Weather: early mist, rain. Script: the state uses Latin/Roman letters for most languages (unverified). City line: "Itanagar".
- Music: slow 84 bpm, pentatonic with a rising line; rhythm: hand drum and bamboo flute-like lead (unverified). No real songs.
- Postcard line: "Itanagar: the first light, and the last landslide."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
The China border, McMahon Line and any claims; insurgency; hornbill-beak headgear and costumes; tribal stereotypes (nose plugs, tattoos, "head" jokes); rice beer; religious sites (Tawang, shrines); the Donyi-Polo faith; mithun as sacrifice; dog-meat jokes; thukpa (GTK); recoil ropes, gliding and a timed-light door (taken).
