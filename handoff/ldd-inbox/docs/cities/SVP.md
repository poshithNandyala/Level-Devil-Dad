# SVP · Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), Andaman & Nicobar Islands (bonus): city bible

Research for Level Devil Dad, chapter 20 (bonus). Facts carry a link or say (unverified). Checked against
`docs/MECHANICS.md` and the Done tables. Kavaratti (KVT) already did the lagoon, see-through water, reef diving,
swimming with air, tuna, dolphins, crabs, palms, boats and a lighthouse beam, so SVP takes **land and town**
ideas only. Also taken: logs and rollers (`amr03-logs`, kit), coconut crabs (KVT-07 crabs), geysers (kit),
caves in the dark (AMR-07), peacocks (GNR-06), turtle nests (KVT-01), tides (DMN, PDY-06). Hooks kept for SVP in
this batch: the saw mill's carriage and cut, Ross Island's roots and deer, the mud volcano, saltwater crocodile
warnings, parrots at dusk, two monsoons, the island's mixed Hindi.

## 1. Identity
- To islanders: a small town of hills and harbours where everyone came from somewhere (Bengal, Tamil Nadu,
  Kerala, Andhra, Ranchi) and the common tongue became Andaman's own Hindi; "mainland" is a word you use daily.
- Visitors come for: Ross Island's ruins and deer, Corbyn's Cove, North Bay, the ships to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock)
  and Shaheed Dweep (Neil), Baratang's mud volcano and limestone caves, Mount Harriet.
- Proud and laughing at: "the ship is late" as a way of life, two monsoons a year, the mainland cousin who asks
  if they need a passport.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Government ships and private catamarans between islands (boats taken); town buses and autos
  on hilly roads; bikes rented by tourists. Use only the jetty waiting hall and the ticket queue (CHN-02 is a
  queue: keep different).
- **Food.** Fish curry and rice in Bengali, Tamil and Malayali styles side by side; grilled fish and lobster at
  the beach; local fruit (unverified specifics).
  - Hidden-item candidates: **machhli-bhaat (fish curry rice)** (first choice), **grilled fish on a stick**,
    **banana fritter** (unverified local name).
  - Traps: a fish-fry stall where the oil spits only on the side of the pan nearest the cook.
- **Markets.** Aberdeen Bazaar; the fish market at the jetty (DMN-06 fish market is taken: weight gag is out).
- **Weather.** Two monsoons, southwest (May-Sept) and northeast (Nov-Dec) (unverified months); sudden
  downpours; humid green.
- **Animals.** Spotted deer on Ross Island (introduced; tame; unverified origin), saltwater crocodiles (warning
  boards near creeks and some beaches, unverified locations), parrots, monitor lizards (unverified).
- **Crafts.** Padauk timber furniture (red hardwood, unverified), shell craft (keep out: collecting shells is
  restricted, unverified).
- **Sports and games.** Football on town grounds (taken), kayaking (boats).
- **Festivals.** Island Tourism Festival (winter, unverified), Durga Puja and Pongal and Onam side by side
  (taken elsewhere; leave). Independence/Netaji days: no gags.
- **Habits and sayings.** "Mainland", "ship kab aayega?", local-born islanders called "pre-42" families
  (unverified), Hindi with Bengali, Tamil and Telugu words.

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
**1. Namaste, Sri Vijaya Puram** · tour · Harbour, Aberdeen Bazaar, a far volcano. Betrayal: a sudden island
downpour; the town's steep lanes run with a sheet of water that pushes you downhill for as long as you stand in
the lane (flowing water as a slope push, not a flood). Second try: wait under an awning. Closest: TVM-02 floods,
PNJ gangway slope; different: shallow sheet flow only on lanes, timed with the cloud.

**2. Aari (The saw mill)** · standard · Chatham-style carriage. Expect: ride the log carriage. Betrayal: the
carriage passes the band saw again and again, each pass slicing one plank off the side of the log; the plank you
stand on is the next to go (it slides off sideways onto the conveyor). Second try: stand on the centre of the
log. FIX: the dad in the centre is there when the last pass halves the log. Act: a sorting floor of planks
sliding off, sorted by colour (padauk red vs pale). Door: the timber stack door: it opens when the stack is
full. Closest: logs and rollers; different: you are on the thing being cut, and the cut is your floor clock.

**3. Jad (The roots)** · long · Ross Island ruins. Expect: walls and floors are solid, roots are scenery.
Betrayal: every brick wall that looks solid crumbles when you land; the roots that look like decoration hold.
Second try: stand on roots. Second betrayal: one root is a monitor lizard's tail (unverified animal; or a
hanging root that swings). Door: the ballroom's empty doorframe, held up only by roots. Closest: crumbling floors;
different: a visual rule (brick vs root) the player learns once.

**4. Hiran (The deer)** · standard · Ross Island deer. Expect: animals as hazards. Betrayal: the deer follow
whoever is holding food; you picked up a fritter at the jetty, so they follow you, and they nudge you off
ledges. Second try: drop the food (stand still and Dad eats it). FIX: the dad who drops it near a ledge has the
herd stop exactly there. Door: the jetty, where the guard says "Hiran ko khana mat do". Closest: chasers
(dogs, crows), `herd` ending; different: you choose to be chased by holding something; they nudge, not bite.

**5. Jalki (The mud volcano)** · "how would I know" · Baratang cones. Expect: hot lava-like danger. Betrayal:
the mud is cool and safe to wade (unverified fact; design fiction if not); what kills is the bubble that swells
under the ground near the cone and pops a crust you stood on. Second try: walk *through* the mud, not the crust
beside it. FIX: the dad who wades waits too long and the cone grows round him (sets). Door: a mud cone that
grows into a step. Closest: geysers (kit), mud you can't jump from (GNR-06); different: crusts that pop beside
the mud, and the safe thing is the mud.

**6. Magarmachh (Crocodile warning)** · standard · A creek with a warning board. Expect: a log to jump on.
Betrayal: the board reads "Crocodiles" and points at the creek, so the obvious fear is the water. The water is
too shallow; the croc is on the bank in the sun, drawn as a log on the bank. Second try: stay in the shallow
water. Second betrayal: when you're in the water the board's arrow turns (it's on a hinge in the wind) and now
points to where it really is: the croc slides in. Door: the board itself folds down as a bridge. Closest:
`fake` floors, logs; different: a warning sign that's honest and points in a moving direction.

**7. Tote (Parrot Island at dusk)** · long · Dusk over mangroves. Expect: birds as hazards. Betrayal: at dusk
the flock lands on the trees; a tree with birds bends low (birds are a weight); branches you need are high
until the birds come. Second try: wait for dusk. Second betrayal: they leave all at once at a boat's horn and
the branch springs up (spring is taken: make it lift slowly and leave you on top of a high branch you can't get
down from). Door: the last tree, reached only bent. Closest: TVM bending palms (KVT-07 bend); different: a crowd
of birds bends the tree by time of day.

**8. Do Monsoon (Two monsoons)** · "how would I know" · The same street in two seasons. Expect: rain from the
west. Betrayal: the stage plays twice; the second monsoon comes from the other side (northeast), so every awning
that kept you dry the first time now funnels water on you. Second try: shelter under the other awnings. Door:
the road from the first pass is the river in the second. Closest: none with wind-rain direction (wind is kit;
here it's only where the water falls).

**9. Choona (Limestone caves)** · standard · Lit caves. Expect: dark (AMR-07). Betrayal: the drip from each
formation is a drip timer; a drip that lands on you is harmless but the guide's lamp shows each pillar's shadow
growing as a step. No: shadows taken. Use instead: formations that ring when knocked; each knock drops a drip
that wets a tile into a slide. Second try: walk past without knocking. Caution: weak; keep only as a reserve.

**10. Ship Late Hai (The ship is late)** · marathon · Jetty waiting hall. Expect: a queue (CHN-02). Betrayal: the
announcement says "ship late"; everyone sits on their luggage and the luggage stacks become the floor; when the
ship is announced, everyone stands at once and takes their luggage. Second try: be on the jetty edge when the
call comes. Second betrayal: the gangway is the second ship's, not yours. Door: the ticket window, which opens
only after the ship has gone. Closest: CHN-02 pots saving places; different: the crowd's belongings as terrain,
and the announcement flips the stage.

**11. Note Ki Tasveer (The view on the note)** · "how would I know" · North Bay viewpoint (unverified note).
Expect: a plain hill. Betrayal: Dad holds up the old note and the view on the note shows a path the hill hides;
only from the exact spot the note's picture was taken does the hidden ledge line up with the visible one. Second
try: walk to the spot marked by the viewing bench. Door: the lighthouse door, which appears in the note only.

**12. Dweep (The island, finale)** · finale · Callbacks only to SVP: the saw that slices your log, the Ross
Island roots vs walls, the deer that follow the food, the cool mud and the popping crust, the croc board that
turns, the luggage floor that stands up. New twist: you carry the food the deer want through the saw mill.

Picks: 1 + 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12. (9 and 11 are reserves.)

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky `#7EC8E3` (clear tropical), hills `#2E6F40` (forest green), tiles `#A8442E` (padauk red wood), accent
  `#F6E27F` (sun on the harbour), second accent `#5C4B3B` (volcano mud).
- Skyline: green hills with houses on stilts and tin roofs, a harbour with cranes, a far cone of a volcano, ruins
  with roots, a white lighthouse. Ground cap: red laterite road with a white kerb.
- Weather: brilliant sun broken by sudden heavy rain, two directions; evening parrot clouds.
- Local script name: श्री विजय पुरम (Devanagari).
- Music: 104 bpm, a mix: Bengali khol, Tamil thavil and a Hindi folk dholak taking turns over a steady beat; major
  key; ship horn as the downbeat effect (unverified "island sound"; this is a design choice).
- Postcard line: "Ship is late. Deer are early. Dad is in the mud."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
The protected tribes (never: no Jarawa reserve, no trunk-road convoy, no North Sentinel, no "tribal" anything);
Cellular Jail (freedom-struggle memorial: no gags, no stage); the 2004 tsunami and earthquakes; Japanese occupation
and penal colony history as jokes; collecting coral or shells; feeding wildlife (only as the rule the guard
says); real ship and ferry operators; navy and air bases.
