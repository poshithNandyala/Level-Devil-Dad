### 1. Ghanti (The mule's bell) — slot 07, the SOUND beat
- **Place:** the Tsomgo road in cloud; a mule man stands with a decorated mule above a slip (Tsomgo's kiosks offer "joy rides on decorated yaks and mules": [Wikipedia: Tsomgo Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsomgo_Lake)). GTK-09 owns riding the yak; here the animal is a landmark only, never ridden.
- **Rule:** a bell rings where the mule stands; an animal standing in the cloud proves road under it. The kerb rope here stops and its loose end hangs **down** into the gorge (the far post went with the slip), which is the tell that the gap is not a jump-width: six tiles of road are gone and the detour is a stone step up the bank, where the bell is.
- **Decision:** In the cloud, walk to the sound, not along the rope.
- **Obvious play:** jump where the rope stops (07's rule). The gap is six tiles. Death `gtk-badal`, play "the rope's end hung down".
- **Obvious fix:** wait under the kiosk awning for the lift (`thau`'s rule). This is a standing cloud (a fixed bank, drip edge drawn); after 8 s the mule man leads the mule on and the bell fades up the road; the player who waited now walks blind to find it. Same cause, different play (allowed in 07 per LENGTH-2: the cloud edge is 07's one hazard).
- **One-move answer:** up the bank step toward the bell, pass behind the mule, down the far side onto the road where the rope resumes.
- **Closest:** GTK-09 yak (a ride on a fixed loop); HYD-06 golconda-echo-clap (sound tells distance to a wall); the room's rope (a line shows the edge). Different: nothing is ridden, nothing is clapped; a sound source is proof of floor, and the tell at respawn is the bell ringing (TROLL-BIBLE D1, cause visible).
- **Time:** 12-15 s.
- **Line:** "Ghanti suna? Tyahi bato chha." | "Hear the bell? That's where the road is." (U; fallback Hindi "Ghanti ki taraf chalo, Dad!").
- **Archetype:** sound-as-tell landmark (B-number not checked); the stale-rule law (the stage's own rope rule is made wrong once, with a tell).
- **Source:** Tsomgo mules V ([Tsomgo Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsomgo_Lake)); bells on Sikkim pack animals U.

### 2. Tapkan (The drip line) — slot 07, the SHELTER beat
- **Place:** a stretch of shop fronts with awnings; the cloud is in; a rain burst is coming (Gangtok weather "can change abruptly from bright sunshine and clear skies to heavy rain within a couple of hours": [Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)). The rain band is a KILL hazard like SVP-08's rain, sweeping the open road every 10 s.
- **Rule:** in the cloud you cannot see the awnings, but every awning drips, and the drips make a dark line on the paving. A drip line on the road means cover overhead.
- **Decision:** When the rain comes, stand on a drip line, not under the awning you remember.
- **Obvious play:** run for the awning you saw before the cloud rolled in; you stop one tile short or long (it is drawn one tile off from where the paving pattern suggests). Death `gtk-barsat` (new cause: "The rain. You were a tile outside the awning.").
- **Obvious fix:** stand still where you are when the rain starts (`thau`'s wait): no roof. Same cause, different play.
- **One-move answer:** walk to the drip line and stop on it; wait out the band; walk on.
- **Closest:** `thau` (wait under a visible awning for the cloud), SVP-08 (dry at the lee end of an awning, a wind read), TVM-04 coconut on stoppers (a roof that drops). Different: the roof is unseen; the proof is on the ground; no wind, no lee side.
- **Time:** 12-15 s (two bands; the second has two drip lines and one is a gutter spout, a wider wet patch, honest and visible).
- **Line:** "Thopa khasne thau, chhat chha." | "Where the drops fall, there's a roof." (U; fallback Hindi "Jahan boond, wahan chhat.").
- **Archetype:** shelter read by its trace (B36 shelter trap inverted: the shelter is honest, only its location is hidden).
- **Source:** weather V ([Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); awning drip lines are plain physics.

### 3. Chhat ko dhaar (The balcony you cannot see) — slot 07, the twist on 2
- **Place:** further along the same street; houses whose upper floors overhang the footpath (U).
- **Rule:** a drip line also means something **low** overhead. Where a drip line crosses a gap in the road, a full jump hits the balcony and drops you; a tap-jump fits. Every gap under a balcony is built tap-width (2.4 tiles, MECHANICS tap hop), so the low hop always suffices.
- **Decision:** Under a drip line, jump low: the fog hides the ceiling, not the floor.
- **Obvious play:** jump where the rope stops with a full jump (07's rule): head meets the balcony, Dad falls into the gap. Death `gtk-badal`, play "hit the balcony".
- **Obvious fix:** run-jump to clear faster: the longer arc meets the balcony sooner. Same cause, a third play.
- **One-move answer:** tap-jump at the drip line.
- **Closest:** TVM-08 snake-boat-low-bridges (duck on a boat as a bridge comes), B25 super-jump under a ceiling (FREE), AMR-01 full-stomach-short-jump (jump size set by state). Different: the ceiling is unseen and fixed, the tell is on the ground, the choice is jump size, nothing is ridden and nothing is eaten. Candidate 2 teaches "drip line = roof"; 3 turns the same tell against the player (TROLL-BIBLE law 2: the countermeasure becomes the vulnerability).
- **Time:** 10-12 s (two balconies; a third gap with no drip line is a full jump, to test the rule).
- **Line:** "Tauko bachau, Dad!" | "Mind your head, Dad!" (U; fallback Hindi "Sar bachao, Dad!").
- **Archetype:** B25 ceiling (FREE per SVP-IDEAS-2), with the fog as the reason the ceiling is unknown.
- **Source:** overhanging floors U; MECHANICS jump sizes quoted from SVP-IDEAS-2 (tap 2.4, hold 3.75, run 5.6).

### 4. Nali ko garjan (The roaring drain) — slot 07, alternate to 1 (build 1 or 4, not both)
- **Place:** a hill-side stretch where the kerb rope runs on the drain side (the footpath is on the gorge side and has the slip, so the rope is on the hill side here). Gangtok roads have the footpath on one side and the drain on the other ([Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); the drain is covered with gratings.
- **Rule:** a grating is floor; where a grating is missing the water in the drain roars (open water is loud, covered water is quiet). The fog hides the hole; the roar marks it.
- **Decision:** Along the rope, hop where the water is loud; the quiet gratings hold.
- **Obvious play:** follow the rope and walk the gratings: through the missing one. Death `gtk-nali` (new cause: "Into the drain. The loud one had no grating.").
- **Obvious fix:** cross to the footpath side to avoid the drain: the footpath has the slip and no rope there. Death `gtk-badal` (a different trap).
- **One-move answer:** stay on the rope side; tap-hop every roar.
- **Closest:** candidate 1 (sound proves floor; here sound marks the hole, its mirror), 03/04's jhora (a visible water hazard), fake floor with a tell (taken: here nothing looks like floor; there is simply no floor and the cloud hides that).
- **Time:** 12-15 s.
- **Line:** "Paani garjyo? Hop, Dad!" | "Water roaring? Hop, Dad!" (U; fallback Hindi "Paani ki awaaz: kood, Dad!").
- **Archetype:** sound-as-tell (as 1).
- **Source:** road cross-section V ([Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); gratings U.

### 5. Aina (The hairpin mirror) — slot 07, the MOMENTUM beat
- **Place:** a hairpin on the loop road with a convex mirror on a post at the bend (standard on Indian hill roads; U for Gangtok specifically).
- **Rule:** the mirror shows the far leg of the bend before you can see it. In the mirror a cloud bank is rolling toward the bend. You have about four seconds of clear bend.
- **Decision:** Read the mirror and go now; do not stop at the bend to look.
- **Obvious play:** stop at the bend to see what the mirror shows: the bank arrives, the bend's two gaps are in cloud, the walk on is blind. Death `gtk-badal`.
- **One-move answer:** run the bend as soon as the mirror shows the bank; the gaps are tap hops you take in clear air.
- **Closest:** `ghumti` (bank behind, keep moving) and `jaldi`, its 4.6 s momentum replay. This is the same keep-moving rule with a new tell (the bank is ahead and seen only in the mirror). LENGTH-2 allows 07 one momentum replay and it is spent on `jaldi`; if 5 is built, cut `jaldi` (net +0.5 s) or keep both and accept the second replay. Honest verdict: a momentum beat by definition, not a new decision.
- **Time:** 5 s.
- **Line:** "Aina ma herau: badal aayo!" | "Look in the mirror: the cloud's coming!" (U).
- **Archetype:** momentum replay (TROLL-BIBLE stage-shape rule for marathons).
- **Source:** U.

### 6. Bijuli ra garjan (Count the thunder) — slot 07, the long beat
- **Place:** an open stretch of road with no overhang (a retaining wall on the hill side, the gorge on the other), then a covered bus shelter, then another open stretch. Monsoon storm inside the cloud (monsoon rains are "intense torrential": [Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)).
- **Rule:** lightning shows as a white pulse through the fog; thunder follows N seconds later; N shrinks with each flash (the storm is coming closer). When N reaches zero the strike lands on the open road (a KILL flash across the uncovered stretch), then the storm passes and N grows again. The open stretch takes about three seconds to cross.
- **Decision:** Count flash-to-thunder; cross the open stretch only while the count is longer than the crossing; shelter while it is shorter.
- **Obvious play:** cross when the flash lights the road (light = you can see the gaps): the count is already two, the strike comes mid-stretch. Death `gtk-bijuli` (new cause: "The strike. You counted nothing.").
- **Obvious fix:** shelter at the first flash and wait for the storm to go: the rolling bank behind (`ghumti`'s hazard) reaches the shelter after 12 s, and the player leaves blind. Death `gtk-badal`, a different trap, with the bank drawn coming.
- **One-move answer:** count at the first flash (N = 5: go), cross, shelter, count again (N = 1: stay), let the strike pass, count (N = 4: go).
- **Closest:** HYD-06 echo clap (a sound delay you make), RNC-08 storm-over-falls-tallest (lightning strikes the tallest thing), KVT-03 lighthouse beam (a timed window). Different: nothing you make, no tallest rule; a countdown read by ear that shrinks and grows, and the fog is why your ears are all you have.
- **Time:** 15-20 s.
- **Line:** "Ek, dui, teen... garjan!" | "One, two, three... thunder!" (U: Omniglot's Nepali numbers page not fetched; Hindi fallback "Ek, do, teen... garaj!").
- **Archetype:** a timer the player reads (B6 deadline family: the deadline is audible, not drawn).
- **Source:** flash-to-thunder distance is plain physics (about three seconds per kilometre); Gangtok storms V.

### 10. Bhari gaadi (The taxi that is about to fill) — slot 06 primary
- **Place:** the shared-taxi stand of 06 (share-jeeps are Gangtok's public taxis for longer runs: [Gangtok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); taxis leave when full (U as a rule, common practice).
- **Rule:** each taxi shows its passengers through the windows; a taxi with nine aboard pulls out the moment the tenth sits; one with three stays. Nothing times a departure except the count.
- **Decision:** Cross in front of the taxi with the empty seats, never the one with one seat left.
- **Obvious play:** wait on the last step for the taxi to pull out (06's `modh` rule): the half-empty one never does; the player crosses in front of the full one when a passenger arrives. Death `gtk-taxi`.
- **Obvious fix:** go after the first taxi leaves (06's `upar2` lesson says the first is not the all-clear): the second is at nine. Death `gtk-taxi`, a different play.
- **One-move answer:** read the windows; cross in front of the three-seat taxi; the queue of passengers walking to the nine-seat one is the tell.
- **Closest:** `upar2` (a count of two taxis), CHN-02 pot-queue places, "wait for the taxi" (06 own). Different: the count is of passengers inside, read through glass, and it predicts the departure rather than reporting it.
- **Time:** 10-12 s.
- **Line:** "Nau jana bhayo: aba janchha!" | "Nine in: it'll go now!" (U).
- **Archetype:** a counted threshold (count-routing family is PAT-08's bricks; this counts people to predict a mover, not to route).
- **Source:** share-jeeps V; fill-and-go U.
