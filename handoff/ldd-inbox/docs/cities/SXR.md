## 1. Identity

- To the people who live there: the lake city of the valley: Dal and its houseboats and shikaras, chinars,
  kahwa from a samovar, bread from the neighbourhood baker every morning, and a long, proud craft tradition.
- Visitors come for: a shikara ride, a night on a houseboat, the gardens on the lake's edge, the tulips in
  spring, Gulmarg's snow and slopes, Sonamarg's meadows.
- Proud and laughing: hospitality measured in cups of kahwa, a shikara called "the Gondola of Kashmir"
  **[F]** [Dal Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dal_Lake), and a winter in which the fire-pot under the
  pheran goes everywhere with you.

## 4. Language pack (Kashmiri + Urdu/Hindi + English)

Mix: about 45% Kashmiri (Koshur), 35% Urdu/Hindi, 20% English. Source: Wikivoyage
[Kashmiri phrasebook](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kashmiri_phrasebook) **[F]**, which gives *Toh chiv
väri?* (how are you), *ähansa* (fine), *meharbani* (thank you), *ên* (yes), *na* (no), *yi kyāh chu?* (what
is this?), *be chus* (I am), *çê kyāh gaçhiy?* (what do you want?), *ba-lai la-gai* (please) and the numbers
above. WhatsApp spelling drops the marks: *toh chiv vaaray*, *meharbani*, *yi kyah chu*. Greetings the
phrasebook lists are religious salutations, so the game uses "Kyah chhu?" (CITIES.md) and *toh chiv vaaray*.
