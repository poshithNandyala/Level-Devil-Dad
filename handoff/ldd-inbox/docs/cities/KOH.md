# KOH · Kohima, Nagaland: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = every word appears in at least two listed sources, U = unverified (fallback given). Nothing reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities: SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis; ITA = sunrise, shadow, bricks, cane bridges, paddies, mithun; **KOH = steep lanes and roofs, the log drum, the wooden cart, smell (axone), the festival ground, the meadow**; IMP = floating ground, polo; AIZ = stilt houses, stairs, bamboo poles; AGT = palace water, rubber, rickshaws; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons.

Sources: [WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima) · [W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival) · [W-DZK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dz%C3%BCkou_Valley) · [W-NAG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagamese_creole) · [LH-Nagamese](https://languageshome.com/English-Nagamese.htm) · [Quora: basic Nagamese](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-basic-Nagamese-phrases-for-people-who-travel-to-Nagaland) · [WTC](https://www.worldtranslationcenter.com/blog-posts/nagamese-for-travelers-essential-phrases-for-the-adventurous-soul) (low trust, used only where cross-checked)

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a town on steep ridges, called Kewhi-ra by the Angami (the land where the kewhi flower grows) ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); over 90% Christian, Sunday church, hill lanes where everyone walks; Nagamese is the language everyone shares and English is official ([W-NAG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagamese_creole)).
- Visitors: the Hornbill Festival at Kisama (1-10 December, ~12 km out, a "festival of festivals" with a morung for each community, [W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)); Dzukou Valley and Japfü peak; Khonoma and Bara Basti villages ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)).
- Pride and laugh: food that is boldly flavoured (smoked pork, fermented bamboo shoot, the king chilli chutney, [WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); "everyone will help a visitor" ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); the power goes off a lot ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima), Razhü Pru entry) and nobody minds.

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
1. **Kisama Heritage Village / Hornbill Festival** · a morung (traditional house) exhibit for each community; crafts, food stalls, indigenous games, Naga wrestling, traditional archery, rock concert ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)) · row of high-gabled thatched houses with carved posts · betrayal: finale ground; do not make dancers hazards.
2. **Dzukou Valley** · 2,438 m meadow with dwarf bamboo; seasonal flowers May-September; icy stream (name "cold water" is one reading) ([W-DZK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dz%C3%BCkou_Valley)) · rolling green with a white lily and a thin stream · betrayal: concept 6. The valley is claimed by two states: draw only the meadow, never a boundary or fire (wildfires also hit it).
3. **Japfü peak** · 3,048 m, second highest in Nagaland ([W-DZK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dz%C3%BCkou_Valley)) · a long dark ridge, rhododendron red · seed: ridge backdrop.
4. **Khonoma (the green village)** · terraced paddies (CITIES.md) · stepped bright terraces · seed: scenery; terraces are taken (GNR-02).
5. **Kohima Village / Bara Basti** · "second largest village in Asia", traditional Angami life ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · tight clusters of tin and thatch roofs on a slope · betrayal: concept 3.
6. **Naga Bazaar** · "nearly anything living and moving" on sale ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a covered market of baskets and bamboo · seed: baskets; do NOT make a joke about animals sold.
7. **State Museum** · rare artefacts, models of traditional sports equipment ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a plain building with a low roof · seed: the tug-of-war rope and top models.
8. **Kohima Zoo** · Blyth's tragopan (state bird) and mithun ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a shy forest bird, red and grey · seed: shy bird (taken as an idea; scenery only).
9. **Cathedral on Aradura Hill** · biggest church of its kind in the region; city views ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a tall pointed building on a hill · scenery only; no hazard.
10. **Hill roads and landslides** · monsoon landslides on Dimapur-Kohima and Kohima-Mao ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a hairpin with a slip · concept 1 uses the slope.
11. **Naga shawls** · hand-knitted on verandas, tourists are welcomed to learn ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · red-black stripe band · scenery/colour bands only.
12. **Pulie Badze and Jotsoma** · peak overlooking Jotsoma ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · a low peak with a pole · seed: meadow ridge.
AVOID: the WWII cemetery and Garrison Hill, the Kohima Stone inscription (political), headhunting, Dzukou's boundary dispute.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Steep narrow lanes, city buses ₹20, local taxis ₹100-200, "line taxis" from BOC junction to Kisama and Kigwema; Maruti Omni shared cars from Dimapur ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)). Shared-taxi-leaves-when-full is SHL's; KOH uses the cart, the lanes and the log instead.
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **galho** (rice porridge, CITIES.md) · (2) **axone** (fermented soybean, famous smell, CITIES.md) · (3) **bamboo shoot** (fermented, with pork, [WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)). Recommend **galho**: a warm bowl that is easy to draw, and the smell hook is saved for axone as the mechanic. Also Naga chutney and a king-chilli pickle ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); the chilli is AMR/`mirchi` territory, so use only as a jar.
- **Weather.** Cool winters (frost possible, snow on the highest ridge), 18-27 °C summers with heavy rain, landslides ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)). Cold mornings and after 2 pm ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)).
- **Animals.** Mithun, Blyth's tragopan, the hornbill (festival's namesake, [W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)). Hornbill-gliding is taken (glider).
- **Crafts.** Wood carving, shawls, bamboo, blacksmith work ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival) lists carving, painting, sculpture).
- **Sports and games.** Traditional archery, Naga wrestling, indigenous games at the festival ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)); rope games (unverified); football and rock music are common (unverified).
- **Festivals (backdrop only, respectful).** Hornbill Festival; each community has its own sacred agricultural festivals ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival): "the Nagas consider their festivals sacred"). Never a hazard; no costume jokes; no weapons as props.
- **Habits and sayings.** Power cuts; walking everywhere; hospitality; Sundays quiet ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); Nagamese "bhal ase" answers almost any greeting ([Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-basic-Nagamese-phrases-for-people-who-travel-to-Nagaland)).

## 4. Language pack (Nagamese + English)
Nagamese is Assamese-based, written in Roman script, spoken by nearly all in Nagaland as the shared language ([W-NAG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagamese_creole)). Words cross-checked in LH-Nagamese and Quora/WTC: moi (I), toi/tumi/apni (you), ahi (come), jabi/jabo (go), khabi (eat), bhal (good), bhal ase (it's good/fine), hoi (yes), nai (no), kotha ase (where is), ki (what), kiman (how much), naam (name), bhat (rice/meal), gari (vehicle), rasta (road), thik thak (fine), loghat (with), poisa (money). Map greeting: use "Kemon ase?" only after a native check (CITIES.md also doubts it); the safe greeting is "Apuni kineka ase? / Bhal ase?" (U for spelling). Hindi at most 2 lines; none used here.

5. Dad, toi bhat khabi? | Dad, will you eat? (V)

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the first lane is a long staircase; the "railing" is a bamboo rod that rolls: grab it and you roll down a step; skip it and you walk. 

1. **Kath ka Gari (wooden cart downhill)** · hook: teaser "Wooden Cart Downhill" (CITIES.md) and Kohima's steep lanes ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)); (unverified: kids ride self-made wooden carts down lanes) · expect: ride downhill, brake at the bottom · betrayal: the cart accelerates; you steer by leaning (left/right = shift weight); one lane turn has a "hairpin" with a stall; brake pad only works when off a bump. · second rule: brake before the first bump, not after; second betrayal: the second hairpin has a slick patch where braking spins you. · door: the cart stops at a garage door only if you bump it with the left front. · length: marathon (3 checkpoints, each a different hill) · closest: bangle roll with momentum JAI-05 and bus roof slide TVM-02; different: a downhill cart with a lean-steer and a brake, no jump.
2. **Dhol Gach (log drum haul)** · hook: carved log drums hauled by crews (CITIES.md; Hornbill, [W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)) · expect: ride the log as a moving platform · betrayal: the log moves in surges on each crew shout; between shouts it rolls back a pace; step off during a roll-back and you hit the lane; the crew is fixed and cannot be sped. · second rule: step on only after the shout; second betrayal: the last pull is a double shout. · door: the drum hut. · length: long · closest: rope ferry DMN-05 (you mash to haul) and JAI-02 puppet beats; different: you are a passenger; the beat belongs to others, no input changes it.
3. **Chaal-Chaal (stacked roofs)** · hook: hillside houses stacked on slopes (Kohima Village/Bara Basti, [WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · expect: roofs are scenery · betrayal: you must cross on tin roofs of different slopes; a roof's slope tilts you; thatched roofs hold but tin roofs are loud and a rooster wakes the dog (dogs taken) so use: tin roofs ring and wake the household who open a door below. · second rule: cross thatch first; second betrayal: thatch has a chimney with warm air that lifts. · door: the top roof's skylight. · length: long · closest: chawl climb BOM-06; different: sloped roofs that slide you.
4. **Axone Handi (the pot with the smell)** · hook: fermented soybean, famous smell (CITIES.md) · expect: a cooking pot is a pot · betrayal: Dad picks up the pot; its smell cloud grows when the lid is off; the crows and dogs hate it (they keep away), but the guide NPC cannot stand it and goes away so no hint line; keep the lid on and the pot is cold and no one is scared. · second rule: lid off at one place, on at the other. · second betrayal: the cat (not dog) loves it. · door: a kitchen door opening for the smell. · length: "how would I know" (the smell rule has no sign) · closest: cocoon RPR-06 (hawks like, hornets can't); different: a carried scent that selectively repels.
5. **Rassi Tan (tug of war)** · hook: indigenous games at the festival ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)) · expect: stay out of it · betrayal: the rope is the bridge; two teams pull and the middle flag drifts toward the stronger side in rounds; you walk the rope in the drift. · second rule: the stronger side changes when you pass the middle; second betrayal: a third team joins. · door: the winners' stand. · length: standard · closest: rope bounce PNJ-05 and mash-to-haul DMN-05; different: a team contest decides the platform's motion by rounds you do not control.
6. **Dzü-Kou (the cold meadow)** · hook: icy stream and flower meadow ([W-DZK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dz%C3%BCkou_Valley)) · expect: stroll · betrayal: flowers open in a wave left to right; an open lily is a stand; the wave reverses at the stream; the stream's cold slows you (move at half speed; recover on land). · door: the valley rim. · length: standard · closest: blinkers (kit) and heat-less icy ground DMN-03; different: the wave is a travelling platform pattern and the cold slows you.
7. **Bans Jhula (the bamboo seesaw)** · hook: indigenous games at the festival ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)); seesaw play (unverified as a Naga game) · expect: ride the seesaw yourself · betrayal: the one who must fly is the guide NPC, not Dad: you jump on the low end, the NPC on the high end is thrown up to a ledge and drops a ladder; if you jump from the wrong spot he overshoots and the ledge empties. · second rule: jump from the lowest tile, not the middle; second betrayal: the second seesaw has the NPC on the wrong end. · door: the ladder's top. · length: standard · closest: `salaami` (Dad is tossed on to a gate) and cannon launch; different: it is the helper who is launched, by your jump, and you wait below.
8. **Pakar (the wrestling ring)** · hook: Naga wrestling is a festival sport ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)) · expect: a fight · betrayal: nothing hits you; the ring's edge is the enemy: an NPC wrestler walks straight at you and pushes you out; step aside at the last tile and he walks out himself; the second wrestler feints. Show only a sport with kind clothes. · second rule: step to the side only when his shadow lengthens; second betrayal: the third wrestler is already outside, pulling. · door: the ring's rope gap. · length: standard · closest: bull ending and shove BOM-03; different: the opponent is removed by your dodge, not fought.
9. **Frost Gali (the dawn lane)** · hook: frost on cold mornings ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · expect: ice is slippery · betrayal: puddles frozen at dawn look like glass; they hold a walker and break under a hop; the lane's sun moves up the slope, so by the top it is water and slippery mud. · second rule: walk the ice early, hop the water late; second betrayal: one patch of ice is under a roof and never thaws. · door: the cold gate. · length: "how would I know" (the thaw rule has no sign) · closest: melting ice DMN-03; different: freezing and thawing driven by the sun's slope position.
10. **Thaan (the loom shed)** · hook: hand-knitted and woven shawls on verandas ([WV-KOH](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Kohima)) · expect: threads are scenery · betrayal: rows of vertical warp threads form walls; as the weaver lifts the shed stick, alternate rows open; you cross each row only through the open gap, which alternates left/right. · second rule: the weaver's beat is the same every row; second betrayal: the last row opens twice. · door: the cloth's hem. · length: standard · closest: thread BPL-04 (a thread you may never cross) and blinkers; different: rows open in alternation by a weaver's rhythm.
11. **Kisama Din (festival day)** · hook: ten days, a morung for each community ([W-HBF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornbill_Festival)) · expect: wander · betrayal: each of five houses along the yard has its own floor rule (a drum floor that sinks, a ladder that lifts, a hearth that warms, a post that sways, a gate that counts); you learn one and the next differs; the exit is the first house's back door. Show no sacred rite; it is the architecture only. · second rule: the rule of house N is the opposite of house N-1; second betrayal: house 5 uses both. · door: the first house's back door. · length: marathon (3 checkpoints) · closest: roulette PNJ-06; different: five unrelated rule sets in one yard.
12. **Subah Kisama (finale)** · calls back only Kohima ideas: the cart (1), the log (2), the roofs (3), the axone pot (4), the rope (5) and the seesaw (7) in one run to the Hornbill ground. · door: the biggest morung gate. · length: finale.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky cool grey-blue **#8FA6B8**; hills dark pine **#2B4A3A**; tiles red clay/brown **#7A3E2B**; accent shawl red **#C0392B** (unverified, from descriptions).
- Skyline: tin roofs stacked on ridges, a tall pointed church on a hill, thatch gables with crossed horns (avoid horn headgear), Japfü ridge behind.
- Ground cap: packed earth with fern edges; frost white at dawn.
- Weather: cold dawn, rain in summer, drifting mist.
- Script: Latin (Nagamese is written in Roman, [W-NAG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagamese_creole)); local name line: "Kewhi-ra".
- Music: 92 bpm, pentatonic major, with slow-attack vocal harmony (the church choir tradition is strong, unverified) and a log-drum thud on beat 1 only; no real songs.
- Postcard line: "Kohima: steep streets, warm porridge, and the strongest smell in the Northeast."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Insurgency, ceasefire, peace talks; the WWII cemetery and battle; the Kohima Stone; Dzukou's boundary and wildfires; dog-meat jokes; headhunting; tribal stereotypes and warrior costume jokes; weapons as props; beading/dress as comedy; hornbill beak headgear; religious sites as hazards; mirchi as a mechanic (taken); beat-forced hops (taken); terraces (taken).
