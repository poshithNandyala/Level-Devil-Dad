## 1. Identity

- To the people who live there: a high cold desert town at about 3,500 m on the Indus, where water comes
  from snow and glaciers, winters are long, and every guest gets "Julley" and a cup of butter tea.
- Visitors come for: Khardung La, Pangong, Nubra's dunes and double-humped camels, the frozen-river Chadar
  trek, Magnetic Hill, the Indus-Zanskar confluence, Hanle's dark sky, the Ladakh Marathon.
- Proud and laughing: one word (Julley) for hello, goodbye and thank you **[F]**
  [Wikivoyage Ladakh](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ladakh), ice hockey as the most popular sport **[F]**
  [Ladakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakh), and visitors gasping on the first flight of stairs.

## 4. Language pack (Ladakhi + Hindi + English)

Mix: about 45% Ladakhi, 30% Hindi, 25% English: in Leh, Ladakhi is spoken by about 40% and most young
people also speak English and Hindi fluently **[F]** [Ladakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakh). Source
for Ladakhi: Wikivoyage [Ladakhi phrasebook](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ladakhi_phrasebook) **[F]**:
*Jule / Jullay* (hello, goodbye, thank you), *Khamzang?* (are you well?), *Khamzang* (I'm well), *Hago*

(no, polite), *tsapik* (a little), *Dik* (it's OK, enough), *-le* (polite particle), *Zhuks-le* (sit, polite),

## 6. Look and sound

- Palette: sky `#2F6FDB` (deep high-altitude blue), hills `#B89A74` (bare brown mountains), tiles `#EDE6DA`
  (whitewashed mud brick), accent `#F28C28` (dried apricot), second accent `#7EC8E3` (ice blue).
- Skyline: flat-roofed white houses with dark window frames and stacks of fodder on the roofs, a tall
  tapering palace on a ridge (generic, nine storeys), bare brown ranges with snow tops, poplars along the
  Indus. No monasteries, stupas or prayer flags (Avoid).
- Ground cap: gravel road with a white edge; packed dust; on the Chadar, white ice with snow on the edge.
- Weather: hard bright sun, sharp shadows, clear night skies; in winter, snow on the peaks, not falling on
  you (falling snow is SML's).
- Script: Tibetan script for Ladakhi (Bhoti) **[F]** [Wikivoyage Ladakh](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ladakh)
  (written Ladakhi is the same as Tibetan), Devanagari for Hindi.
- Music: 90-105 bpm, Ladakhi daman (drum) and surna (shehnai-like reed) folk feel **[F]**
  [Ladakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladakh), a dranyen-like plucked lute (unverified), pentatonic.
  No monastic chant or long horns.
- Postcard line: "Julley! | Thin air, thick ice and butter tea."
