# GTK · Gangtok, Sikkim: city bible

Research for Level Devil Dad, chapter 21. Facts carry a link or say (unverified). Checked against
`docs/MECHANICS.md` and the Done tables. Taken and left alone: cable rides and the ropeway (Cable kit,
`amr06-zip`, AMR-08), jeeps on hairpins (TVM-07), mist clouds (kit), rockfall and landslide debris (kit, plus
`dmn04-dozer`), melting/slippery ice (DMN-03), steam hiding spikes (TVM-03), sunrise is fine as a backdrop.
Tea goes to DIS (Assam), not Temi. The "floors below the road" idea is claimed here; AIZ ("Ten Floors Below")
must find something else. House-to-house carol groups (Deusi-Bhailo here, husori in Assam) go to DIS. Hooks kept
for GTK: buildings entered from two roads at different floors, short-cut stairs between road loops, MG Marg's
rules and its sweepers, the momo steamer's tiers, churpi, yaks at the high lake, the organic rule, the polythene
ban, monsoon fog that hides the drop.

## 1. Identity
- To Gangtokians: a clean, steep, polite little capital strung along spiral roads, where you walk up stairs to
  visit the neighbour below, and MG Marg is the evening living room.
- Visitors come for: MG Marg, the ropeway view, Tsomgo (Changu) lake and its yaks, Kanchenjunga at sunrise, then
  North Sikkim's Lachung and Yumthang, and Pelling in the west.
- Proud and laughing at: "spit on MG Marg? you'll pay", every house being "ground floor" from some road, the
  momo argument (steamed vs fried), legs of steel from the stairs.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Taxis and personal vehicles are 98% of Gangtok's vehicles; buses under 1%; share-jeeps for
  longer runs; 4WD for the slopes ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)). Jeeps on hairpins are taken:
  use the taxi stand queue instead, and walking.
- **Stairs.** Footpath stairs cut between road loops (unverified density; everyday knowledge). Trap: a stair can
  only be taken down if you started it at the top: half-way down, the next flight is someone's front step.
- **Food.** Momo (steamed in stacked tiers), thukpa, churpi (hard yak-milk cheese chewed for hours), sel roti
  (ring-shaped fried rice bread), gundruk (fermented greens), phagshapa (unverified), cardamom (Sikkim is a
  large-cardamom state, unverified).
  - Hidden-item candidates: **momo** (first choice), **sel roti**, **churpi**.
  - Traps: momo steamer: the cook moves the top tier to the bottom to cook evenly (a rotating stack);
    sel roti: rings fried in a pan, flipped with a stick; churpi: the only block that never breaks.
- **Markets.** Lal Market, MG Marg shops, the organic produce stalls.
- **Weather.** Fog, cloud through the streets, monsoon rain, landslides, rare snow (1990, 2004, 2005, 2020,
  [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)).
- **Animals.** Yaks at the high lakes, red panda (state animal, unverified), Himalayan monal (unverified),
  dogs (taken).
- **Crafts.** Handmade paper from vegetable fibres or cotton rags is a cottage industry
  ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); thangka painting (religious: avoid); carpet weaving
  (unverified).
- **Rules.** Statewide polythene bag ban ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); 100% organic farming
  since 2016 ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangtok)); MG Marg's street rules.
- **Sports.** Football is loved (taken); archery is a Sikkimese/Bhutia sport (taken, RNC-03).
- **Festivals.** Losar, Saga Dawa, Pang Lhabsol, Dashain and Tihar are religious or semi-religious: no gags. A
  winter carnival on MG Marg (unverified name) is safe as a crowd.
- **Habits and sayings.** "Kasto chha?", "dhanyabad", "hajur" (respectful yes), "ali ali" (a little),
  "ekchin" (one moment, unverified).

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
**1. Namaste Gangtok** · tour · MG Marg, stacked roads, a far snowy peak. Betrayal: the street-sweeper's broom
sweeps everything on the ground toward the bin, Dad included, if he stands still in its path. Second try: keep
moving or step onto a bench. Closest: treadmills (kit); different: it follows a sweeping arc and only things on
the ground, timed to the sweeper's swing.

**2. Kun Floor? (Which floor?)** · standard · A house with doors on two roads. Expect: the lift's "G" is the
ground. Betrayal: the lower road's door is "G" too; the buttons count from the road you came in from, and the
door you want is on the other road's G. Second try: take the stairs down, count floors. FIX: the dad who counts
from the top forgets the shop floor that has no door. Act: three houses in a row, each with a different number
of floors under the road. Door: `ground`: the exit is on "G", which is floor 6 from here. Closest: BOM-06 chawl
stairs, AIZ teaser; different: numbering that depends on your entrance (claim it for GTK).

**3. Seedhi (Shortcut stairs)** · long · Stairs between road loops. Expect: Tara says the stairs are faster.
Betrayal: they are faster, but they come out one loop lower than the road you wanted, behind a taxi stand.
Second try: take the stair *up* to the loop above and walk down the road. Second betrayal: a stair ends on
someone's roof garden, which is the only way across. Door: the stair that's a doorstep. Closest: none in
stairs (BOM-06 is wall kicks); different: a topology puzzle on a hillside.

**4. MG Marg ka Niyam (MG Marg's rule)** · "how would I know" · The walking street. Expect: a calm street.
Betrayal: drop anything (a momo wrapper) and a whistle blows: the warden walks to you and you must stand still
till he writes the fine (a long pause while the sweeper comes). Dad drops the wrapper the first time you jump
while holding one. Second try: put it in a bin (walk past a bin). Second betrayal: the bins are emptied on a
round, and a full bin can't take it. Door: the street's end, where vehicles are allowed: a taxi blocks the door
till you've paid. Closest: CHN-02 queues; different: a rule the player breaks by jumping.

**5. Momo Steamer** · standard · Stacked steamer tiers. Expect: a stack to climb. Betrayal: every few seconds
the cook lifts the top tier off and slides it under the bottom; you on the top tier go to the bottom, into the
steam pot's heat. Second try: stand on the second tier; when the top moves, you're on top. FIX: the dad on the
second tier is under the lid when it comes down. Act: two stalls, one rotating up, one down. Door: the
plate: the door is under the last tier served. Closest: rotating platforms (`revolve`); different: a stack that
cycles top-to-bottom.

**6. Churpi (The hard cheese)** · "how would I know" · A dairy stall. Expect: everything breaks. Betrayal: every
floor on this stage crumbles (soft cheese, sel roti, paper), except the grey churpi blocks that never break,
even under a falling weight. They look dull and you skip them. Second try: walk only on churpi. Second
betrayal: the vendor chips one churpi block into chunks for a customer. Door: the yak-hair rope gate (no ropes
as rides: it's a latch). Closest: crumbling floors; different: the dull one is the only safe one.

**7. Badal (The cloud in the street)** · marathon · Monsoon fog. Expect: a blocking mist (kit). Betrayal: the
fog doesn't block; it hides where the road ends; the guide rope along the kerb is the only proof of the edge,
and the rope has gaps where the landslide took the posts. Second try: walk by the rope and stop at each gap.
Second betrayal: when the fog lifts for a moment the road looks complete because a painted wall continues it.
Door: the road reopens when a bulldozer (backdrop) clears it; the door is behind the barrier, which lifts only
when the cloud lifts. Closest: mist clouds; different: fog hides an edge rather than blocking.

**8. Plastic Nahin (No polythene)** · standard · Lal Market. Expect: shopping bags as platforms. Betrayal:
cloth bags hold (they're legal); the thin bags look the same but tear under you, and shopkeepers confiscate
them as you pass (the ban). Second try: step on bags with stitched handles. Door: the cloth-bag stall. Closest:
fake floors; different: two kinds with a readable tell (stitches).

**9. Jaivik (The organic farm)** · "how would I know" · A terrace farm. Expect: the green crops are scenery.
Betrayal: the farmer drives away anything not organic; Dad picked up a mainland packet at the taxi stand
(you saw it in his hand), so the farmer's dog herds him off the terraces. Second try: drop the packet at the
check post (a bin). Door: the farmer's gate, opened once you're "organic". Closest: chasers; different: an item
you carry decides who chases you (close to SVP-04 deer; keep only one of the two if they feel alike).

**10. Tsomgo (The high lake)** · long · Frozen lake with yaks. Expect: slippery ice (DMN-03 taken). Betrayal:
the ice holds; the shore is where it's thin, where yaks walk a fixed circuit; ride a yak's back as it walks
round (it never leaves the circuit). Second try: get off on the far side. Second betrayal: the yak handler
stops the yak for a photo, exactly on the thin shore. Door: the snowfield gate. Closest: JAI-08 elephant ride,
the dummy horse; different: a fixed loop you can't steer, and the stops are for photos. Check it isn't too close
to the elephant.

**11. Sel Roti (The ring bread)** · standard · Frying pan. Expect: rings as platforms. Betrayal: the cook flips
each ring with a stick when it's golden on one side; a pale ring won't be flipped yet, a golden one will. Second
try: stand on pale rings. Door: the basket the rings go into. Closest: RNC-02 dhuska (flipped and scooped):
**too close**; keep only as a reserve.

**12. Kanchenjunga (Finale)** · finale · Callbacks only to GTK: two ground floors, shortcut stairs, the MG Marg
rule, the momo steamer, churpi blocks, the fog with the kerb rope. New twist: in fog you can't see which road
you came in from, so the floor count is a guess unless you counted.

Picks: 1 + 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12. (9 and 11 are reserves.)

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky `#BFD7EA` (cool Himalayan), hills `#3D5A40` (forest slope), tiles `#D9D4C7` (clean paving stone), accent
  `#E07A5F` (rhododendron), second accent `#F4A261` (sunrise on the peak), fog `#ECEFF1`.
- Skyline: stacked hillside houses with coloured roofs, roads looping across the frame, a giant snow massif behind,
  a cable line in the far backdrop only. Ground cap: tiled walking street with planters down the middle.
- Weather: fog banks, monsoon rain, sudden sun, rare snow.
- Local script name: गान्तोक (Devanagari; Nepali).
- Music: 92 bpm, Nepali folk feel, major pentatonic, madal (two-headed hand drum) on a jhyaure-style beat
  (unverified rhythm name), bansuri flute, sarangi.
- Postcard line: "Every house is on the ground floor. Just not your ground."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Prayer flags, prayer wheels, monasteries, chortens and statues (no gags, no mechanics); the border, Nathula and
the army; Dashain, Tihar, Losar, Saga Dawa as traps; landslide deaths; ethnic groups compared or joked about
(Nepali, Bhutia, Lepcha); "chinky" or any look-based comment (slur); real hotels and cafes; the lottery.
