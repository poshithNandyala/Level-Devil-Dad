## 1. Identity

- To the people who live there: the Doon valley town between the Shivaliks and the Mussoorie hills: schools,
  institutes, bakeries, litchi and basmati, blue Vikrams, and the gate to the Garhwal hills.
- Visitors come for: Mussoorie up the hill, Rishikesh's rafting below, Robber's Cave, Sahastradhara, the
  Forest Research Institute, Jim Corbett and the Valley of Flowers further off.
- Proud and laughing: the old "pensioners' paradise" calm (unverified saying), rusks with every cup of tea,
  and an eight-seat Vikram that always takes one more.

| 5 | Jhoola Pul (The swinging bridge) | Suspension footbridge | A swaying bridge sways at random | It sways only with the walkers' step: walk in step with the crowd and the sway grows until it throws you; walk out of step and it calms | Walk on the off-beat | An ox walks on at the far end (slow, heavy step): the beat changes mid-bridge | `jhankaar`: a bell on the far tower rings only when the bridge is still: stand still mid-bridge till it rings, and the door opens | standard | AMR-06 rope bridge, PNJ-05 ropes; here the sway is your own rhythm against a crowd's (resonance) |

| 8 | Phool Dei (Flowers at the door) | Spring flowers on every doorstep **[L]** | Every doorstep is a step | Painted house fronts have painted doorsteps; the children run ahead putting flowers only on real ones, and the flowers wilt fast: keep up with them | Stay just behind the children | One child runs back for more flowers: the markers ahead of you stop for a moment, so stop with them | `deli`: the door is the last threshold; it opens only with a flower on it, and you have the last one | marathon | `fakewin`, `paintwall`; here moving markers (children) say which is real, and they don't wait |

| 11 | Vikram (The blue tempo) | 8 passengers in the back **[F]** | A vehicle at the stand is a fixed step | The Vikram dips with every passenger who gets in and rises as they leave: its roof is low (easy to board) only when it's full | Board when full, ride, get off on the ledge when it empties at the stop | It's route 2: at the fork it goes north or east "depending" **[F]**: read the board, the tilt of the driver's head, or get off before the fork | `sawari`: the door is a seat; the conductor shouts "ek sawari aur" and you are that one: board only on his shout | standard | BOM-03 shove-out, DMN-06 weighing; here the vehicle's height follows its load and is your lift |
