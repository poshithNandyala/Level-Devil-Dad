# DEL · New Delhi: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = every word standard Hindi/English or confirmed in a listed source, U = unverified (fallback given). Nothing here reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities (read with the sibling bibles): SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis, market day; ITA = sunrise, shadow, bricks, cane bridges, paddies; KOH = steep lanes, log drum, cart, smell, loom; IMP = floating ground, deer, polo; AIZ = stacked houses, stairs, helping, silence; AGT = latex, battery rickshaws, palace water; **DEL = a city built on top of itself seven times, ring-shaped New Delhi, fog and horns, tangled wires, children's lane games, lawn rules, lane crowds**.
Deconflicts done after reading the sibling files: pigeon flying (kabootarbaazi) belongs to LKO, so DEL does NOT use it; wrestling/akhara belongs to CHH; pittu/lagori is listed as a hook in PAT.md but not as a stage, so DEL takes it as a stage (PAT builders: leave it); PAT uses fog only as one second betrayal over a bridge, and CHP/CHH/DDN/SML deliberately leave fog unused, so DEL owns fog as sound (horns), not as hiding; battery rickshaws are AGT's; kites, trains, autos, wedding horses are taken (CITIES.md).

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a city that has been built, abandoned and rebuilt many times ([W-HIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Delhi)); New Delhi is the planned part with wide boulevards, roundabouts and colonial mansions, Old Delhi the walled lanes with huge crowds ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu, English and Bengali are all heard, and Punjabis are a large share of the people ([W-HIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Delhi)).
- Visitors: Red Fort, Qutub Minar (73 m), Humayun's Tomb, Connaught Place and the lanes of Chandni Chowk ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); chaat, paranthe and jalebi ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)).
- Pride and laugh: "yaar" after everything ([Intrepid](https://www.intrepidtravel.com/adventures/how-to-sound-like-a-local-in-india/)); traffic where "honking is frequent" and lane discipline is loose ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); winter nights near 0-5 °C and summers over 40 °C ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); a famous street of nothing but parathas ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)).

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (13)
1. **Connaught Place** · a popular shopping centre in the British-planned city of roundabouts ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); concentric ring roads with radial spokes and white colonnades (unverified: [W-CP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connaught_Place)) · concentric white arcades · concept 1.
2. **Chandni Chowk and its galis** · narrow side lanes with old havelis, "extremely crowded" ([Swan Tours](https://www.swantour.com/blogs/top-22-tourist-places-to-visit-in-delhi)); Paranthe Wali Gali sells only parathas ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · a lane of shopfronts with awnings · concept 4.
3. **Overhead wires of Old Delhi** · tangled wires being concealed in a beautification project ([Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/old-delhi-beautification-project-electrician-has-to-deal-with-tangled-wires-angry-bystanders-and-a-looming-deadline-5023537/)) · bundled black cables with sag · concept 6.
4. **Red Fort** · 17th-century palace of Shah Jahan ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · a long red wall with a gate and a lawn · seed: red wall as skyline and door shape.
5. **Qutub Minar** · 73 m, 13th century, intricately carved ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); the world's tallest brick minaret at 72.5 m ([W-HIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Delhi)) · tall tapering tower with balcony rings · concept 10.
6. **The seven cities** · Lal Kot / Qila Rai Pithora (1052/1180), Siri, Tughlaqabad, Jahanpanah, Ferozabad (1354), Purana Qila (the sixth, Sher Shah), Shahjahanabad (the seventh, Old Delhi), then New Delhi inaugurated 1931 ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi), [W-HIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Delhi)); numbering varies by author (unverified) · stacked ruins · concept 5. Show shapes only, never conquests.
7. **Lutyens roundabouts and the lawns** · wide boulevards, roundabouts ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); big public lawns with "keep off the grass" signs (unverified) · neat green circles · concept 7.
8. **Humayun's Tomb** · a vast 16th-century garden tomb ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · memorial: skyline only, no hazard.
9. **Dilli Haat** · the state-run handicraft market ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · rows of thatched stalls · seed: dressing.
10. **Sarojini Nagar, Karol Bagh, Lajpat Nagar markets** · clothing markets ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · racks and tarps · seed: dressing.
11. **Feroz Shah Kotla, Ferozabad** · multi-floored water reservoir and an Ashokan pillar ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · pillar on a ruin · concept 5 (a layer).
12. **Red-and-green DTC buses** · big windows, stop "pretty much anywhere" ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · seed: scenery; buses are taken (TVM-02).
13. **Hop-on hop-off tourist bus** · pre-defined stops, 45-minute interval ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · seed: marathon structure (concept 5).
AVOID as seeds: Parliament, Rashtrapati Bhavan, India Gate and the war memorial, Jama Masjid, Lotus Temple, Akshardham, Raj Ghat, the Yamuna ghats, Jantar Mantar protests (JAI-04 also took Jantar Mantar).

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Metro lines (taken: BLR-03 and BOM trains); DTC buses; yellow-and-green CNG autos, three seats (taken: HYD); cycle rickshaws, now often replaced by e-rickshaws, best for Old Delhi's galis ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)); the Hop-on bus; loose lane discipline and constant honking ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)). Trap: the horn code (concept 2).
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **gol gappa / pani puri**: small round hollow shells with a spicy filling ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · (2) **paratha** from Paranthe Wali Gali ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · (3) **jalebi** ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)). Recommend **gol gappa**: a round, hollow, instantly readable sprite, and no other city has used it; avoid jalebi (RNC dhuska oil) and paratha (flat discs resemble dhuska). Also chole bhature, kulfi, rabri (CITIES.md).
- **Markets.** Chandni Chowk (cloth, wedding wear), Sarojini Nagar, Karol Bagh, Lajpat Nagar, Dilli Haat ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)).
- **Weather.** Winter December-February 0-5 °C at night; summer March-May up to 45 °C; monsoon June-September; "smog season" October-February ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)). Use "winter fog" only; no pollution blame (CITIES.md avoid).
- **Animals.** Nilgai, monkeys (taken), pigeons (LKO). Not used.
- **Crafts.** Block prints, silk and handloom, khadi ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)).
- **Sports and games.** Gilli-danda and lagori/pittu (lane games, [PAT.md](PAT.md) lists them; Wikipedia pages: [lagori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagori), unverified fetch), cricket (taken). Akhara wrestling is CHH's.
- **Festivals.** Republic Day parade and Independence Day (political: do NOT use), Diwali and Holi (taken), Lohri (religious: avoid). None used.
- **Habits and sayings.** "Yaar" everywhere ([Intrepid](https://www.intrepidtravel.com/adventures/how-to-sound-like-a-local-in-india/)); "chhad yaar, koi gal nahin" (let it be, no problem) is a Delhi Punjabi-flavoured phrase ([TOI](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/delhi-times/delhi-so-to-speak/articleshow/164343.cms)); "bhayya" and "bhaisaab" on the Metro ([MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-in/news/other/delhiwale-the-new-dilli-speak/ar-AA28PAHx)); "bhasad machi hai" (it's chaos) ([Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-three-most-used-words-by-Delhi-people)); confident, loud directions (CITIES.md).

## 4. Language pack (Delhi Hinglish + Punjabi flavour + English)
Delhi speech is Hindi-English with Punjabi seasoning. Words confirmed in the sources above: yaar, bhai/bhayya/bhaisaab, chhad yaar, koi gal nahin, bhasad machi hai (mild slang), kya scene hai (CITIES.md). All other words are plain Hindi (V by any dictionary). Avoid slang that is rude or mocks people (the Quora lists contain such words; none used). Written the way Delhi people text.

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the tour walks the Lutyens lawns; the guide's "walk on the path" is true, but the shortcut over the grass is where the sprinklers wait; and the first rotary has five exits, one of which is the way back.

1. **Gol Chakkar (the ring)** · hook: Connaught Place's rings and spokes ([W-CP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connaught_Place), unverified) · expect: run to the door · betrayal: the level wraps: leave the right edge and you arrive at the left; the door is in the ring's "block" with the letter shown on the sign; each lap moves the hazards by one block. · second rule: count laps by the lamp-post numbers; second betrayal: the middle ring runs the other way. · door: the block with the matching letter. · length: marathon (3 rings = 3 checkpoints) · closest: Benz Circle AMR-05 (every direction wrong; speed humps) and clock-hand circular lift; different: the world wraps and the lap count matters, no direction trick. Builders need a wrap-around camera; check cost first.
2. **Kohra (winter fog and horns)** · hook: honking is frequent; winter fog ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · expect: fog hides things · betrayal: the fog hides everything beyond two tiles, but every vehicle announces itself with a visible horn ring; ring colour and spacing tell speed and lane (one long = bus, two short = rickshaw) and the guide teaches the code truthfully once. · second rule: listen for the rhythm, not the loudness; second betrayal: two vehicles honk the same code. · door: the lit toll arch, shown only by its horn. · length: "how would I know" (the code has no sign beyond the guide's first lesson) · closest: mist pits TVM-07 and PAT's fog over a bridge; different: the fog is not a hider but a sound-reading puzzle, with all hazards telegraphed by horn only.
3. **Lagori (seven stones)** · hook: lane game with a stack of seven stones and a ball ([PAT.md](PAT.md)) · expect: dodge the ball · betrayal: after the ball scatters the stones, each stone you step on jumps back into the stack and becomes a step; the thrower aims at your feet on a beat; step in the order of the stack (largest first). · second rule: the stack builds upward, so go up it; second betrayal: the last stone is thrown away from the stack. · door: the top stone's gap. · length: standard · closest: shots (kit) and rice into hills RPR-04; different: your steps build a stairway out of scattered pieces under fire.
4. **Gali Sankri (the squeezing lane)** · hook: crowded narrow galis ([Swan Tours](https://www.swantour.com/blogs/top-22-tourist-places-to-visit-in-delhi)) · expect: crowd = slow · betrayal: shopkeepers pull awnings and carts out as you linger; a lane that is wide when you move narrows to nothing when you stand; running hits a fixed rickshaw. · second rule: walk at the pace of the lane's beat; second betrayal: the exit lane has stalls pulled in. · door: the haveli gate. · length: standard · closest: rising walls (kit) and the shy ledge TVM-04; different: space shrinks by standing, not by time.
5. **Saat Dilli (the seven layers)** · hook: the seven cities ([W-HIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Delhi)) · expect: seven sections · betrayal: each section's floor plan is the previous one's ruins with a new city built on top: old arches are platforms; new walls cover them; the old plan is the safe route. · second rule: remember the last section; second betrayal: the seventh is the first's mirror but on a different scale. · door: the layer's top. · length: marathon (7 layers) · closest: two-world stencils BPL-03 and the `dejavu` ending; different: each layer is built on the ruins of the layer before it, and the answer is the old layout. No conquest imagery.
6. **Taar Jaal (the wire web)** · hook: tangled overhead wires of Old Delhi ([Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/old-delhi-beautification-project-electrician-has-to-deal-with-tangled-wires-angry-bystanders-and-a-looming-deadline-5023537/)) · expect: wires are scenery or manja caps · betrayal: Dad can hang from the cables and traverse hand over hand; one cable in each bundle is live and hums (a wavy line you can see); the guide teaches the hum once. · second rule: swap cable at the knot, not the middle; second betrayal: a knot is a dead end. · door: the pole's ladder. · length: "how would I know" · closest: kite string manja height caps (HYD-05) and arcs (kit), palm climbing KVT-07; different: a hang-traverse verb and a live wire that you can only read by its hum. Needs a new "hang" state.
7. **Ghaas Par Mat Chalo (keep off the grass)** · hook: Lutyens lawns and roundabouts ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · expect: walk through the lawn as a shortcut · betrayal: sprinklers sweep arcs that push you, and a whistle sends you back to the start of the lawn; the path is slower but the sprinkler's arc has a gap. · second rule: walk the paving line; second betrayal: the paving is wet in places. · door: the garden gate. · length: standard · closest: rollers and the wind kit; weak: builders may drop in favour of 10.
8. **Tandoor Daud (the clay oven)** · hook: tandoor cooking, Delhi dhabas (unverified) · expect: climb the wall · betrayal: a giant tandoor wall: Dad sticks to the hot clay for three seconds, then falls; the chef's seekh sweeps the mouth. Draw a cook, not a weapon. · second rule: stick, hop, stick; second betrayal: the wall cools unevenly. · door: the tandoor mouth. · length: standard · closest: wall kicks BOM-06 and melting ice DMN-03; different: a timed cling. Check CHP.md for tandoor before building (not found there).
9. **Chhan (the kadhai spit)** · hook: kachori and jalebi frying in Chandni Chowk lanes ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi), unverified for kachori) · expect: oil is the hazard · betrayal: crossing a puddle makes Dad wet for a few seconds; wet Dad near the kadhai makes the oil spit; dry on the doormat first. · second rule: use the mat; second betrayal: a mat is also wet. · door: the shop counter's flap. · length: standard · closest: boiling sprays and the Pongal pot CHN-01; different: the player carries a "wet" state from a puddle to the stove.
10. **Qutub Seedhi (the Minar spiral)** · hook: the 73 m tower ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)) · expect: climb the stairs · betrayal: each storey is a ring around the tower; the camera wraps so that the door of a storey is behind you; the balconies alternate round and angular (unverified). · second rule: go with the sun side; second betrayal: the top ring reverses. · door: the lantern at the top. · length: standard · closest: concept 1 (shares the wrap engine; pick one), `lightwell`; different: a vertical wrap. Draw the tower as a shape only, no interior.
11. **Rehri Train (the push-carts)** · hook: street vendors' push-carts parked on slopes with a brick under the wheel (unverified) · expect: carts are static · betrayal: kicking the brick starts a cart; carts are chained and roll as a train down the lane; ride, then get off at the stall. Weak; closest KOH cart and BOM-02; builders may drop.
12. **Dilli Darshan (finale)** · calls back only Delhi ideas: the ring wrap (1), the horn code (2), the lagori stack (3), the squeezing lane (4), the wire web (6) and the kadhai spit (9) in a hop-on-hop-off bus loop ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi)). · door: the last stop's gate. · length: finale.
Picks for 9 + the tour (suggested): 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12; drop 7, 10, 11. Marathons: 1 and 5. "How would I know": 2 and 6.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky winter dusk **#A9B7C6**; hills none (flat plain; use red stone walls) **#B5472F**; tiles sandstone **#D2A06A**; accent marigold orange **#F29E1F** (unverified, chosen from the sandstone/fog look).
- Skyline: arched red-stone wall, a tall fluted tower, round Lutyens lawns, black wires hanging between balconies, a white-domed tomb silhouette, honking-vehicle shapes in fog.
- Ground cap: red sandstone paving, clipped lawn edge with a "keep off" board, tarred road with painted edge.
- Weather: winter fog with sodium-lit haze; a hot white sky in the day.
- Script: Devanagari "दिल्ली" ([WV-DEL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Delhi) writes Hindi, Urdu and Punjabi names); Urdu and Gurmukhi names also exist.
- Music: 100 bpm, minor-key with a drone, a dhol-and-tabla hybrid (CITIES.md names the Delhi dhol): dhol on beats 1 and 3, a quiet horn pad; no real songs.
- Postcard line: "Dilli: seven cities, one big honk."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Politics, Parliament, ministries and rallies; Republic Day parade and war memorials (India Gate); pollution blame and "smog" jokes; 1984 and every riot (2020, 1947 migration, 1974); religion (Jama Masjid, temples, Lohri, Ramlila); pigeons (LKO), kites (taken), trains and metro "mind the gap" (taken), autos (taken), wedding horses (taken), mist-as-hider (taken), akhara (CHH); slurs and the rude slang on the Quora lists; brand shops and sweet shops by name; Hinglish film lines.
