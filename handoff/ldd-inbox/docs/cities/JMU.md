## 1. Identity

- To the people who live there: the Dogra city on the Tawi at the foot of the hills, the warm winter capital,
  a bazaar town of dry fruit, kalari kulcha and rajma-chawal, and the start of the long road north.
- Visitors come for: the old palaces and gardens on the Tawi, Patnitop and Sanasar up the highway, Mansar
  and Surinsar lakes, Bhaderwah, Basohli's painting, and the food.
- Proud and laughing: kalari, the "mozzarella of the Dogras" **[F]** [Kalari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalari_cheese),
  a road north that used to take all day and now goes through a mountain, and Dogra warmth that always has one
  more plate.
