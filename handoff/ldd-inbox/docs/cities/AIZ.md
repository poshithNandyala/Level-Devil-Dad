# AIZ · Aizawl, Mizoram: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = words confirmed in at least two listed sources, U = unverified (fallback given). Nothing reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities: SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis; ITA = sunrise, shadow, bricks, cane bridges, paddies, mithun; KOH = steep lanes, log drum, cart, smell, wrestling, loom; IMP = floating ground, deer, polo, mothers' market, fishing; **AIZ = a city built downward (stacked houses, stairway lanes, 60% slopes), politeness and helping (tlawmngaihna), silence, the bamboo-pole dance as a one-off**; AGT = palace water, rubber, rickshaws; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons.

Sources: [WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl) · [W-CHAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapchar_Kut) · [W-TLAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlawmngaihna) · [W-CHER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheraw_(dance)) · [Census: Aizawl](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/27837/download/31006/DH_15_2001_AIZ.pdf) · [Quora: Mizo phrases](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-basic-phrases-in-Mizo-for-people-travelling-to-India) · [translatiz](https://translatiz.com/translation/mizo-to-hindi) (machine translator, used only to cross-check "Ka tha e, ka lawm e") · [Omniglot Hmar](https://omniglot.com/language/phrases/hmar.htm) (a neighbouring language, for "chibai")

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a ridge city at 1,132 m with timber houses on slopes of 60% or more, about 300,000 people, where you take a footpath for vertical trips and the road for horizontal ones ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)); a moral code, tlawmngaihna, means putting the group before the self, helping any guest or stranger first ([W-TLAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlawmngaihna)).
- Visitors: the city's view, Reiek model village, Tam Dil lake, Vantawng falls, Phawngpui "Blue Mountain", Champhai plains ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)), and Chapchar Kut with the bamboo dance ([W-CHAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapchar_Kut)).
- Pride and laugh: the taxi fare charged by the hill, not the kilometre ("1 km uphill costs more than downhill", [WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)); a city of 5,000 taxis where nobody honks (CITIES.md; unverified in fetched sources); everything shut on Sunday except the pakora seller ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)).

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
1. **Aizawl ridge and the stacked houses** · timber houses on 60%+ inclines; Tlawng valley west, Tuirial east ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)); CITIES.md: door at road level, home floors below · a lit hillside of boxes · concept 1.
2. **Stairway lanes and footpaths** · vertical travel is quicker on foot paths than by road ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · long stair run between hairpins · concept 2.
3. **Hairpin roads and landslides** · fares rise if a landslide forces a longer route ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · a zigzag road · concept 6, 9.
4. **Cheraw (bamboo-pole dance)** · 6-8 performers, pairs of long bamboo poles tapped on the ground and struck together while dancers step in and out; Guinness record in 2010 with over 10,000 dancers in Aizawl ([W-CHER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheraw_(dance)), [Census](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/27837/download/31006/DH_15_2001_AIZ.pdf)) · a grid of bamboo pairs on the ground · concept 4 (flagged as close to existing dance/rhythm ideas).
5. **Chapchar Kut** · spring festival (first Friday of March) with dances, songs, games, ethnic food stalls; includes the custom of playfully pushing a boiled egg at each other's mouths ([W-CHAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapchar_Kut)) · a stadium of colour · backdrop only; never a hazard.
6. **Reiek** · a model Mizo village with a hilltop viewpoint, festive at Christmas ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · hut cluster on a ridge · backdrop.
7. **Tam Dil (Tamdil) lake** · natural lake known for fish and prawns, 85 km away; boats sometimes closed as in bad shape ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · small lake with a hut · seed: scenery only (water/boats taken).
8. **Vantawng falls** · 750 ft, Mizoram's highest, in thick bamboo forest ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · tall thin fall · seed: bamboo forest backdrop; falls are taken.
9. **Champhai plains** · the biggest stretch of flat rice fields, edged by smoky hills ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · flat green with a long road · seed: contrast to the city (flat!).
10. **Phawngpui (Blue Mountain)** · highest peak, orchids and rhododendrons ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · blue ridge with fog · seed: backdrop.
11. **Dampa tiger reserve** · leeches on rainy days; tigers and elephants live there ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · wet forest floor · concept 7 (leeches only; no tiger chaser).
12. **Bara Bazar, Millennium Centre, Zarkawt** · the shopping centres; bamboo caps (khumbeu), puan (woven cloth), baskets ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · market on steps · concept 8.
AVOID: Solomon's Temple and churches as set pieces; the border and refugees; drugs; Zawlaidi wine; Rih Dil.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** City buses (1 km ≈ ₹5 in 2012) through the Dawrpui centre; ~5,000 taxis, fares set by uphill/downhill ("no taxi runs by the published rates"), airport trip ~₹800; ten-seat Sumos for intra- and inter-state trips; no buses to the airport ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)). Shared-vehicle-leaves-when-full is SHL's; AIZ's idea is the fare by height (concept 9).
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **bai** (vegetable stew with bamboo shoot, CITIES.md) · (2) **sawhchiar** (rice porridge, CITIES.md; KOH has galho, a similar porridge) · (3) **fried vegetables and pakoras** from Sunday street vendors ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)). Recommend **bai**: a bowl of stew is easy to draw; avoid porridge to stay clear of KOH. Also momos and a Tibetan restaurant ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)), milky tea from a machine (no bars).
- **Markets.** Bara Bazar, Millennium Centre, Solomon's cave (a shopping area name; do not use), Zohanco for shawls, puan, bags; MKVI for bamboo works and the khumbeu cap ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)).
- **Weather.** 20-30 °C summers, 11-21 °C winters; even monsoon is "pleasant and gentle" ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)); landslides block the main road at times. Rain is not this city's hook (SHL's).
- **Animals.** Clouded leopard at the zoo (photo caption, [WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)); leeches in Dampa; hornbill and hoolock gibbon (unverified).
- **Crafts.** Puan cloth, bamboo caps (khumbeu), baskets ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)). Do not use thread as a hazard (BPL-04).
- **Sports and games.** Football and guitars (CITIES.md); cheraw is a dance, not a sport.
- **Festivals (backdrop only).** Chapchar Kut (March), Christmas lights at Reiek ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)). Show the costume parade as scenery only.
- **Habits and sayings.** Tlawmngaihna: courtesy, helpfulness, unselfishness, "group over self"; strangers are given supplies and hospitality ([W-TLAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlawmngaihna)). Sunday is closed ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)).

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the tour road is flat for three screens (Champhai-flat), then the first step down is a roof: you have been walking on top of a house.

1. **Ten Floors Below (the stacked house)** · hook: door at road level, home floors below ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl) 60% slopes; CITIES.md) · expect: walk down a staircase · betrayal: the house is a stack of floors; each floor's staircase is on the opposite side; family rooms have doors you cannot open; the shortest way is through the next house's balcony; you move downward through the level (scrolling down). · second rule: drop only through the open trap with a rope; second betrayal: a balcony is a neighbour's roof, from which the next drop is long. · door: the lowest door opens onto the road again (the loop: "the door at road level"). · length: marathon · closest: `lightwell` (BOM-06 ending) and chawl climb BOM-06; different: a descending level made of rooms, and the ending returns you to road level.
2. **Staircase Lane (the doors)** · hook: stairways as lanes ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: climb · betrayal: house doors open onto the stairs at set times (someone leaving); a door swung open is a platform to jump over; the door that does not open is the one that blocks. · second rule: wait behind the door of the lower step; second betrayal: the same door opens twice. · door: the top house's gate. · length: standard · closest: shutters (kit) and `shy` doors; different: doors on the route that open outwards into your path.
3. **Tlawmngai (everyone helps)** · hook: strangers are helped first ([W-TLAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlawmngaihna)) · expect: crowd = help · betrayal: whenever Dad stops or stumbles, helpers rush in to carry him forward (a shove); he can't refuse; the way is to stay moving so the help never arrives; and to fall deliberately at one spot so the helpers carry him over a gap. · second rule: fall at the marked mat; second betrayal: the helpers carry you back too. · door: the bridge held by helpers. · length: "how would I know" (stopping brings help, with no sign) · closest: `herd` ending and `reel` (carried to a door); different: help is triggered by your stopping and carries you over the gap only where there are helpers.
4. **Cheraw Grid (the bamboo grid)** · hook: pairs of bamboo, stepping in and out ([W-CHER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheraw_(dance)), [Census](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/27837/download/31006/DH_15_2001_AIZ.pdf)) · expect: dance rhythm · betrayal: the poles are in cross pairs laid on the ground; the pair next to you opens as the last one closes; the beat speeds up after the third row; there is a pair that stays open as a trap (the beat says "open", the poles do not). · second rule: step on the second beat of the pair; second betrayal: the gap in the middle is the wrong one. · door: the stadium gate. · length: standard · closest: sword dancers on a count (kit), dance rings GNR-07, `JAI-02` beats; DIFFERENT? close. Builders may drop it for a different Aizawl-only idea; show it as practice poles with children, never as people at risk.
5. **Shanti Road (the quiet road)** · hook: nobody honks, traffic waits (CITIES.md) · expect: silence = safe · betrayal: with no honks, you get no warning; the only tell is the headlights sweeping the far ridge before the vehicle reaches you; the guide can only say true things. · second rule: look at the far ridge; second betrayal: a headlight on the near side. · door: a gate that opens when the last light passes. · length: "how would I know" · closest: scooters TVM-03, signals BLR-04; different: the warning is a visual signal from another place on screen (the far ridge) and the rule is "no sound".
6. **Dhalan (the 60% incline)** · hook: houses on 60%+ inclines ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: slopes = ramps · betrayal: the floor is a long steep slope; running up is slow, down is fast; at the foot is a drop; jumping while descending carries you farther than expected. · second rule: walk down; second betrayal: a steeper section hides under a tarp. · door: the road below. · length: standard · closest: tilting gangway PNJ and mud GNR-06; different: the slope's grade changes speed and jump length, no push.
7. **Jonk (leech season)** · hook: Dampa leeches after rain ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: wet forest = safe · betrayal: leeches inch along the leaves toward Dad's heat; one that touches sticks and slows him until he stops at a salt rock; they climb only while he stands. · second rule: keep moving; second betrayal: a leaf bridge is all leeches. · door: the salt-rock gate. · length: standard · closest: ant bridges RPR-07 (come to you and bite); different: a clinging effect that slows you and is removed by salt.
8. **Puan (the woven cloth)** · hook: puan cloth ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: a patterned floor · betrayal: a floor woven in stripes whose pattern shifts one band at each step; the colour of the band you step on is the next band's colour (no colour gating: the colour tells where the next solid band is). · second rule: read ahead; second betrayal: the last band is a black one. · door: the cloth's edge. · length: standard · closest: roulette floors PNJ-06 and thread BPL-04; weak, builders may drop.
9. **Fare Meter (uphill costs more)** · hook: taxi fares by incline and landslide ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: ride up · betrayal: a taxi meter ticks by height gained; Dad must keep the meter low by choosing flat paths; each step up adds coins; a landslide forces the longer route and a second meter. · second rule: pay at the checkpoint; second betrayal: the flat route adds a second landslide. · door: the taxi stand. · length: long · closest: tolls/queue CHN-02; different: the cost is by altitude. Needs a coin/score mechanic that does not exist yet; builders may drop.
10. **Veng Veng (the neighbourhood run)** · hook: Zarkawt, Chanmari, Dawrpui, Electric Veng ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: one street · betrayal: each of eight vengs has its own micro-trick for two screens (a stair, a door, a queue, a market, a quiet road, a slope, a leech tree, a taxi). · length: marathon · closest: roulette; different: eight small ideas from this city's list in one run (use ideas 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 once each in tiny form).
11. **Bara Bazar Steps** · hook: bazaar on stairs ([WV-AIZ](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Aizawl)) · expect: browse · betrayal: stalls on steps; oranges (unverified) roll down; stall-holders cover the step with a tarp as you climb. · length: standard · closest: tarp TVM-01; weak, may drop.
12. **Chapchar Kut Raat (finale)** · calls back only Aizawl ideas: the stacked house (1), the stair doors (2), the helpers (3), the quiet road (5) and the slope (6), one run through the festival night; no dancers at risk. · door: the stadium gate. · length: finale.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky evening violet **#6E6A9E**; hills deep green **#2F5A47**; tiles timber brown **#6B4A32**; accent window-light amber **#F5B942** (unverified).
- Skyline: hundreds of boxy houses stepped down the ridge, tin roofs, a ridge-top church spire (silhouette only), lit windows at night.
- Ground cap: stone steps and concrete with fern edges.
- Weather: soft mist, mild.
- Script: Latin (Mizo is written in Roman letters, unverified in fetched sources); game line "Aizawl".
- Music: slow 70 bpm, major pentatonic, with soft guitar and layered choir-like pads (CITIES.md says guitars and choirs); bamboo clap on beat 2 only (not beat-forced hops). No real songs.
- Postcard line: "Aizawl: stairs for streets, no honking, and everyone helps."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Border, refugees, drugs; rice beer or Zawlaidi wine; churches and temples as set pieces; Rih Dil; the bamboo flowering and famine history; dance as a hazard to dancers; pre-Christian rites; thread as hazard (BPL-04); floors-below idea is GTK's teaser name (CITIES.md): AIZ owns it, GTK must not.
