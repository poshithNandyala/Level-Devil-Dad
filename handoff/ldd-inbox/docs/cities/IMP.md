# IMP · Imphal, Manipur: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = every Meitei word checked in the Wikivoyage phrasebook or another listed source, U = unverified (fallback given). Nothing reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities: SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis; ITA = sunrise, shadow, bricks, cane bridges, paddies, mithun; KOH = steep lanes, log drum, cart, smell, wrestling; **IMP = floating ground (phumdi), the deer that steps lightly, polo ponies, the mothers' market, fishing**; AIZ = stilt houses, stairs, bamboo poles; AGT = palace water, rubber, rickshaws; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons.

Sources: [WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal) · [WV-Meitei](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meitei) · [Wikibooks greetings](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Modern_Meitei/Greetings) · [W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake) · [W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park) · [W-IMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ima_Keithel) · [W-PONY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manipuri_pony) · [W-YUBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yubi_lakpi) · [Quora: Thagatchari](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-good-and-simple-words-in-Manipuri)

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a flat valley capital at about 800 m ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) around Kangla, the old fort; Meitei is the main language and English is widely understood ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)); women run the great market, and the matrilineal tradition means "women and girls will not be gawked at" ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)).
- Visitors: Ima Keithel, Kangla, the world's oldest polo ground, Loktak lake and its floating national park ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal), [W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake)).
- Pride and laugh: Manipur claims polo, the only floating national park, and a market of 5,000-6,000 women ([W-IMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ima_Keithel)); the joke is that the deer's feet are lighter than anyone's schedule.

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
1. **Loktak Lake and phumdis** · largest freshwater lake in the NE, 250-500 km², avg depth 2.7 m; phumdi = floating mass of plants, soil and rotting matter; people live on them in "phumshangs" ([W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake)) · green-brown thick mats with a hut · betrayal: concepts 2 and 3.
2. **Keibul Lamjao National Park** · the only floating national park; phumdi 0.3-1.22 m thick, ~20% above water; the sangai (brow-antlered deer) lives here ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) · wet grassland with a deer · concept 1.
3. **Phumdi in the monsoon** · edge phumdi grounded in the dry season; in the monsoon it submerges for days and then re-floats; animals move to Pabot, Toya, Chingjao hills ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) · concept 3.
4. **Ima Keithel** · women-only market, 5,000-6,000 vendors, pagoda-style roofs, in three buildings, 16th-century origin ([W-IMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ima_Keithel)) · long hall with three-tier roofs and stalls in rows · concept 5.
5. **Imphal Polo Ground and Marjing statue** · world's oldest polo ground; the world's tallest polo-player statue ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · a white oval ground, a rider on a rearing pony · concepts 8, 10.
6. **Manipuri pony** · 11-13 hands (112-132 cm), used for sagol kangjei, a polo older than the modern kind; ponies play the whole match (no changes) ([W-PONY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manipuri_pony)); riders use short mallets · small sturdy pony with a straight face · concept 8.
7. **Kangla Fort** · ancient capital, entry only through the west gate; gardens and boats; locals picnic ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · a wide moated enclosure · concept 7.
8. **Samban Lei Sekpil** · world's tallest topiary, 50 ft, 35 steps ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · tiered green hedge cone · concept 6.
9. **Royal boat at the State Museum** · a 78-foot royal boat in the open gallery ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · long narrow boat · seed: boat as scenery only (boats are taken).
10. **Manipur Zoological Garden** · sangai, 55 species ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · scenery.
11. **Game Village / sports complex** · built for national sports events ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · scenery.
12. **Paona Bazaar** · bamboo goods and a South-East Asian feel ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · basket stacks · scenery.
Yubi lakpi (coconut snatching) exists ([W-YUBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yubi_lakpi)) but is tied to a religious festival, so do not use it. Also avoid the Khongjom memorial, Kangla's temple, Shree Govindajee, Sanamahi shrines and churches as set pieces.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Minivans on common routes, tuk-tuks asking ₹100 for ₹10-20 trips ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)), state buses from the terminal, shared jeeps to the "Mao gate". Trap: the tuk-tuk's fare (do not use; auto idea is HYD).
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **chak-hao** black rice (and chak-hao kheer) (CITIES.md; rice varieties incl. black rice sold at Ima, [WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · (2) **singju** (salad, CITIES.md) · (3) **eromba** (CITIES.md; strong smell, so keep KOH's smell hook there). Recommend **chak-hao**: a dark bowl with a purple sheen is easy to draw. Local chilli "umorok" ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)), bamboo-shoot preparations ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)).
- **Markets.** Ima Keithel; Paona Bazaar for bamboo ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)).
- **Weather.** Monsoon peaks June-July; October lush; winter can drop below freezing; 800 m ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)). Phumdi swings with the water level ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)).
- **Animals.** Sangai (dancing deer, state animal), Manipuri pony, python and blue-crested lizard in the park ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)). Deer-as-chaser is not used.
- **Crafts.** Kauna reed and bamboo craft, hand-loom phanek and inaphi ([W-IMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ima_Keithel)).
- **Sports.** Polo (sagol kangjei), pony racing ([W-PONY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manipuri_pony)). Mukna wrestling and thang-ta are avoided (KOH has wrestling; thang-ta is a weapons art).
- **Festivals.** Loktak Day, 15 October ([W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake)); Sangai festival (state tourism; unverified in sources fetched). Never show Ras Lila, Lai Haraoba, Cheiraoba rites, or Yaoshang.
- **Habits.** "Khurumjari" is a respectful greeting that literally means bowing or homage ([Wikibooks](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Modern_Meitei/Greetings)); parting is "see you again" rather than goodbye ([Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-good-and-simple-words-in-Manipuri)).

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the welcome path runs through Kangla's west gate; the gate only opens inwards, so the way out is the way you came.

1. **Sangai Steps (follow the deer)** · hook: the sangai on firm ground within a phumdi ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) · expect: follow the deer on to the mat · betrayal: a deer walks ahead and its feet land on the firm patches (the floating mat is thin elsewhere and tears under Dad); at a gap the deer leaps and Dad cannot. · second rule: wait for the deer to circle back; it only turns at the last firm patch; second betrayal: Tara says "go straight". · door: the hill (Pabot) edge. · length: long · closest: ant bridges RPR-07 (they come to you) and `footsteps`; different: an animal tests the path and you follow its steps only as far as your jump allows.
2. **Phumdi Dock (mats that join)** · hook: mats drift and merge ([W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake)) · expect: jump between mats · betrayal: mats drift slowly in different directions; two mats touching become one with a solid seam; jump too early and you land on a seam still soft. · second rule: wait for the "click" when two mats touch; second betrayal: a third mat arrives from behind. · door: the hut on the biggest mat. · length: long · closest: floating crates BOM-04, dropping/shy floors (kit); different: mats merge into one bigger solid, so waiting makes a bridge.
3. **Resurface (the monsoon cycle)** · hook: phumdi grounds in dry season, floats in the monsoon ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) · expect: solid ground · betrayal: the rain meter climbs; mats separate from the bed; the ground you walked on floats off and tilts; high hills (Pabot/Toya/Chingjao, [W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) are the checkpoints. · second rule: cross while grounded; second betrayal: the dry-season mat re-grounds just before the hill. · door: Chingjao hill. · length: marathon (3 hills = 3 checkpoints) · closest: tides DMN-05/PDY-06, floods TVM-02; different: ground detaches and re-attaches, not water going up.
4. **Net Cast (the fisherman)** · hook: lake fishing by phumshang dwellers ([W-LOK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loktak_Lake)) · expect: nets are scenery · betrayal: a fisher on a boat throws a round net that lands in a circle and is pulled closed; step out of the circle before it closes; the circle's centre is where you were when he started. · second rule: keep moving; second betrayal: he throws where you will land. · door: the net is the door when it is pulled open. · length: standard · closest: TVM-06 nets dip, `catch`; different: a thrown circle that closes.
5. **Sasta Sabse Pehle (cheapest first)** · hook: Ima Keithel stall rows, prices ([W-IMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ima_Keithel)) · expect: cross the stalls nearest first · betrayal: each stall shows a price; only stepping on them in rising-price order is safe; the others close their shutters and drop. Show no customer scam. · second rule: price signs reshuffle each row; second betrayal: one price is in Meitei numerals (ama, ani, ahum: taught by the guide before). · door: the biggest roof's hatch. · length: "how would I know" · closest: roulette PNJ-06, colour-gated floors; different: order by a number shown on each stall.
6. **Samban Lei (the trimmed hedge)** · hook: the 50-ft topiary with 35 steps ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · expect: climb the steps · betrayal: a gardener's shears trim one tier at a time from the top; the tier you stand on is the next to go; leaves regrow after a count; climb as one tier regrows. · second rule: climb behind the shears, not ahead; second betrayal: shears at the bottom. · door: the topiary's crown. · length: standard · closest: dhuska flip RNC-02, sweep SHL-9; different: a trimmer that removes the tier above or below.
7. **Pashchim Dwar (west gate only)** · hook: Kangla's entry is only through the west gate ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · expect: take the near door · betrayal: doors in the east wall open only outwards; only the west gate opens in; the path to the west gate runs around the moat. · second rule: go round; second betrayal: the moat's far bank is a fake. · door: the fort's inner gate. · length: "how would I know" (the gate rule has no sign) · closest: `backdoor` and `shy` doors; different: one-way gates whose direction is the rule.
8. **Chukker (the pony plays on)** · hook: Manipuri ponies play the whole match without changes ([W-PONY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manipuri_pony)) · expect: gallop · betrayal: Dad rides a pony with a stamina bar: gallop tires it, a walk rests it; at the end of each chukker a water trough; a pony that is out of breath stops. · second rule: walk the soft ground; second betrayal: the last chukker has no trough. · door: the Marjing-shaped statue. · length: marathon (7 chukkers, 3 checkpoints) · closest: dummy horse JAI-06 and elephant JAI-08; different: stamina and rest, a steed that is not auto-running.
9. **Thambal (lotus leaves)** · hook: lotus (thambal) in the park's aquatic flora ([W-KLNP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keibul_Lamjao_National_Park)) · expect: stand on leaves · betrayal: dew beads on each leaf roll to the lowest point and push you; a leaf tilts toward the heavy side. · second rule: stand at the rim, not the centre; second betrayal: the next leaf's drops roll the other way. · door: the biggest bloom. · length: standard · closest: tilting gangway PNJ and mud; different: rolling drops that push.
10. **Mallet (the polo ball)** · hook: polo ground ([WV-IMP](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Imphal)) · expect: avoid the riders · betrayal: Dad is hit like a ball by a rider's short mallet; the direction depends on where you stand on the tile; aim up to a ledge. · second rule: stand at the tile's lip; second betrayal: the second rider is the other team. · door: the goal-hoop. · length: standard · closest: `cannon`, `salaami`; different: hit by a mallet whose angle is your position.
11. **Ngaa Ing (the bamboo weir)** · hook: fishing with bamboo traps (unverified in sources; common on Manipur lakes) · expect: swim in · betrayal: a V-shaped bamboo funnel leads in; once inside, the exit is hidden behind the way in; Dad is the fish. · second rule: swim the outside of the funnel; second betrayal: a second funnel waits. · door: the trap's cage lid. · length: standard · closest: `trapdoor`; different: a funnel built to be easy to enter and hard to leave.
12. **Loktak Sandhya (finale)** · calls back only Imphal ideas: the deer's path (1), docking mats (2), the cast net (4), lotus leaves (9) and the weir (11) in one run across the lake at dusk. · door: the hut on the biggest mat. · length: finale.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky pale gold-green **#CFE3D3**; hills blue-grey **#4F6D7A**; tiles reed brown **#8A7A4F**; accent lotus pink **#D86C8A** (unverified, chosen from the lake and valley).
- Skyline: the pagoda-style market roof, a long low lake horizon, a white polo-ground oval with a rearing pony statue, round huts on mats.
- Ground cap: reed mat over dark water; moss at the edges.
- Weather: mist on the lake at dawn, monsoon rain streaks.
- Script: the Meitei script (Meitei Mayek) and Bengali script both used ([WV-Meitei](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meitei)); the game line "Imphal" in Latin letters.
- Music: slow 80 bpm, pentatonic, with a pung-style hand-drum (CITIES.md) played gently on beat 1 and 3 only (no beat-forced hops); a flute (unverified). No real songs.
- Postcard line: "Imphal: a lake that floats and a market run by mothers."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
The conflict and any reference to 2023 or since; insurgency, AFSPA, curfews, border/Moreh; the Women's War and 1891 war memorials; the Kangla temple, Govindajee, Sanamahi shrines, churches; Ras Lila, Lai Haraoba, Cheiraoba, Yaoshang and yubi lakpi; dog/horse smuggling; drugs; rice-wine brands; mocking the sangai or the pony; thang-ta weapons; wrestling (KOH); smell hooks (KOH); floating crates and "sink when you stop" sandbar (taken).
