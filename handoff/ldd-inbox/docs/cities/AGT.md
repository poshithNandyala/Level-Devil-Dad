# AGT · Agartala, Tripura: city bible

Status: research draft. Every fact has a link or is marked (unverified). Language lines: V = every word checked in a listed source, U = unverified (fallback given). Nothing reuses an id in `docs/MECHANICS.md`.
Hook split across the seven cities: SHL = rain gear, roots, caves, taxis; ITA = sunrise, shadow, bricks, cane bridges, paddies; KOH = steep lanes, log drum, cart, smell, loom; IMP = floating ground, deer, polo, mothers' market; AIZ = stacked houses, stairs, helping, silence; **AGT = the sticky-then-solid latex of rubber, battery and pedal rickshaws, bamboo and cane weaving, pineapple, palace gardens and the water palace**; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons.
Overlap decisions: reflections and mirrors (taken three times) are not used at all, even at Neermahal; Unakoti and the Tripura Sundari temple are left out of mechanics (CITIES.md avoid, religious).

Sources: [WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala) · [W-NEER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neermahal) · [W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura) · [Omniglot Kokborok (PDF)](https://omniglot.com/charts/phrases_kokborok.pdf) · [Tripuri phrases](https://en.everybodywiki.com/Common_phrases_in_Tripuri) · [Kokborok, Substack](https://skyfallashes.substack.com/p/speaking-of-kokborok) · [WV-Bengali](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Bengali_phrasebook) · [Kokborok language paper](https://ijcrt.org/papers/IJCRT2610068.pdf)

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- Locals: a plains city of 400,000 on the Haora river, next to the Bangladesh border, where pedal rickshaws are the main way around ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)); Bengali is the main street language, Kokborok the main tribal language, Hindi and English are common ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala), [paper](https://ijcrt.org/papers/IJCRT2610068.pdf)).
- Visitors: the white Ujjayanta palace with its three domes, pools and Mughal-style gardens (built 1899-1901, [WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)), the water palace Neermahal on Rudrasagar lake, the sanctuaries and lakes ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)).
- Pride and laugh: Tripura is India's second-largest rubber producer after Kerala ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)); the state fruit is the Queen pineapple ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)); "Tripura has seven of India's 15 free-ranging primate species, the most of any state" ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)); a city that floods every monsoon and carries on ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)).

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
1. **Ujjayanta Palace** · two storeys, three high domes (the central 86 ft), tile floors, carved ceilings, pools, fountains and floodlights; until 2011 the state assembly ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala), [W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · white block with three onion-like domes and a garden pool · betrayal: concept 6; also the door shape. Draw as a generic white palace, no signboards.
2. **Neermahal (Twijilikma Nuyung)** · the largest water palace in India, built 1930-38 in Rudrasagar lake at Melagarh, 53 km out; 24 rooms, two stairways down to the lake, hand-powered boats from the ghat, a Water Festival boat race and swim in August ([W-NEER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neermahal)) · domed pavilion rising out of flat water · concept 7.
3. **Rudrasagar and Gumti lakes** · thousands of migratory waterfowl in winter ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · flat water, bird lines · seed: birds as dressing.
4. **Dumboor (Domboor) lake** · 48 islands in green forest, birdwatching and water sports ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · scattered green humps in a lake · concept 8.
5. **Rubber plantations** · Tripura is second to Kerala for natural rubber; tea is the other cash crop ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)); latex cups on trees and rubber sheets hung to dry (CITIES.md) · straight rows of grey-brown trunks with small cups · concept 1.
6. **Queen pineapple fields** · the state fruit; jackfruit and pineapple top the horticultural products ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · spiky rosettes on a slope · concept 5.
7. **Bamboo and cane craft** · "cane and bamboo artwork is available"; bamboo and cane forests mix with deciduous trees; handicrafts include hand-woven cotton, wood carving, bamboo ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala), [W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · woven mats and baskets · concepts 3, 4.
8. **Sipahijala sanctuary** · woodland with an artificial lake and botanical and zoo gardens ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)); 7 primate species in the state ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · seed: scenery; no monkey mechanics (`monkey` ending and `monkey` kit are taken).
9. **Jampui Hills** · hill road, once orange plantations, peaceful views ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · scenery; oranges used by ITA.
10. **Unakoti** · ancient rock carvings and murals in a landscape of mountains and waterfalls; a Shaivite pilgrimage site ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · seed: the only safe use is a generic cliff-and-waterfall backdrop with no carved figures; do not make it a mechanic or a joke (CITIES.md avoid).
11. **Haora river and the monsoon city** · the river floods during the monsoon and the city can flood ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · scenery; floods are taken.
12. **Agartala main street at evening** · chai, popcorn, street food ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · lit stalls · concept 9 (the adda circle).
AVOID as mechanics: Tripura Sundari at Matabari, the Chaturdasha temple and Kharchi, the Jagannath and Lakshmi Narayan temples, the mosque, the Venuban Buddha temple, the 1971 war memorial, Akhaura check post.

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Pedal rickshaws are the principal mode (₹5-25); battery rickshaws called "tomtom" are the cheapest; auto rickshaws ("tempu") on fixed routes with an authorised seating of 3 passengers who squeeze in; city buses ₹5-10, 7 routes, low frequency; airport autos by zone fare ₹200-400 ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)). Traps: tomtom battery (concept 2), rickshaw rank (concept 11).
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **Queen pineapple** (state fruit, [W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · (2) **chakhwi** (Tripuri bamboo-shoot stew, CITIES.md; WV-AGT also lists gudok, chakhwi and wak mosdeng among tasty local dishes) · (3) **gudok** (Tripuri dish, [WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala), contains fermented fish berma in CITIES.md's "mui borok"). Recommend **Queen pineapple**: an unmistakable shape, state-proud, and no other city uses it. Also tea, chai and popcorn on the main street ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)).
- **Markets.** Main-street evening stalls; handicraft shops for cane and bamboo ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)).
- **Weather.** Humid subtropical; the hottest months have heavy rain; monsoon flooding; a short mild winter from mid-November to early March (about 18 °C), best time September to February ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)).
- **Animals.** 7 primate species; elephants, gaur, clouded leopard, barking deer in the forests; ~300 bird species ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)). Not used as chasers.
- **Crafts.** Bamboo, cane, wood carving, hand-woven cotton (risa), ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)).
- **Sports and games.** Football and carrom (unverified for Agartala, widely Bengali); fish farming is a major activity ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)). Real clubs are on the avoid list.
- **Festivals (backdrop only).** Garia (CITIES.md), Neermahal Water Festival with a boat race in August ([W-NEER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neermahal)); Kharchi is religious and left out. Never a hazard.
- **Habits.** Adda (long street-corner talk, unverified), "squeeze in by little adjustment" in autos ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)), rain is routine.

## 5. Twelve stage concepts
Tour (01) betrayal: the palace lawn path has a pool whose far edge is the tile the guide says is safe; the first fountain jet that goes up lifts the tile you were about to step on (stand off the jet's base).

1. **Latex Round (the tapper's dawn)** · hook: rubber trees with latex cups and drying sheets ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura), CITIES.md) · expect: white drips are harmless · betrayal: a cup that fills overflows white latex on the path: wet latex is sticky (it slows you like tar), dry latex is a firm rubber floor you can stand on (after a count of 4); stepping on the wet one is the trap; waiting is the answer. · second rule: step only on grey, not white; second betrayal: a cup tips as you pass above and re-wets a floor you thought dry. · door: a hanging rubber sheet that turns solid. · length: "how would I know" (the wet/dry rule has no sign) · closest: tar (kit) and melting ice DMN-03; different: a liquid that is a trap now and a floor later, driven by time rather than by you.
2. **Tomtom (the battery rickshaw)** · hook: battery rickshaws ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: ride across · betrayal: each rickshaw is a moving platform with a battery gauge; it slows as the gauge drops and dies in the middle of a gap; charging stands on the far side restart it; the cheapest-looking rickshaw has the lowest battery. · second rule: change rickshaw at the stand; second betrayal: the next rickshaw is already on a low gauge. · door: the charging stand. · length: standard · closest: bus TVM-02, autos HYD-04, `reel`; different: a moving platform with a finite gauge you must read and transfer between.
3. **Chatai (the bamboo mat)** · hook: bamboo mats (CITIES.md teaser "Bamboo Mat Roll-Up") · expect: the mat is the floor · betrayal: a vendor unrolls a mat ahead of you at his own pace; behind you it is rolled up again; stop and the floor under you rolls; the vendor stops when the guide says "wait". · second rule: do not jump over the edge of the roll; second betrayal: a second vendor rolls from the other end. · door: the rolled end. · length: standard · closest: painted path DMN-07 (floor made as you go) and treadmills; different: the floor is unrolled by a person and rolled up behind you by another.
4. **Bet (the cane weave)** · hook: cane craft ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: a lattice floor · betrayal: strips alternate over and under; only the top strip holds; the top flips each cell as in a real weave. · second rule: count cells from the edge; second betrayal: the cells at the edge are over-over. · door: the basket's mouth. · length: standard · closest: roulette PNJ-06; different: a height pattern (over/under), not a colour.
5. **Anarosh (the Queen pineapple slope)** · hook: pineapple fields ([W-TRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura)) · expect: spiky rows are scenery · betrayal: pickers toss pineapples hand to hand down a human chain across the slope; the thrown fruit arcs through your lane; catching one makes it a step; the last picker throws one too many. · second rule: stand in the thrower's gap; second betrayal: a thrown fruit lands as a platform that rolls. · door: the basket at the foot. · length: standard · closest: coconuts TVM-04 and plank DMN-04; different: a thrown chain whose arcs you can use.
6. **Fountain Court (Ujjayanta)** · hook: palace pools, fountains and floodlights ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: spouts are platforms · betrayal: fountains rise in a sequence shown by the floodlights' beams (not colours); the jet rises where the beam last pointed; the pool tiles have a pattern showing the order. · second rule: read the beam, not the jet; second betrayal: two beams at once. · door: the central dome. · length: long · closest: spouts (kit), roulette PNJ-06; different: the beam shows the future jet, a telegraph you must interpret.
7. **Neermahal (the water palace)** · hook: hand-powered boats from the ghat, stairways down to the lake, 24 rooms ([W-NEER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neermahal)) · expect: a boat ride and a palace tour · betrayal: the boat docks at the landing only when the lake is low on the stair; a wake from each boat covers one step; the rooms are long and the open-air theatre has a stage that lifts. · second rule: climb the wet steps first; second betrayal: the palace's terrace garden hides a gap. · door: the theatre curtain. · length: marathon (boat, landing, rooms, terrace, 3 checkpoints) · closest: ferry DMN-05, tides DMN-05; different: wakes cover steps one at a time as the boat approaches. No reflections.
8. **Dumboor Forty-Eight** · hook: a lake with 48 islands ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: any island will do · betrayal: islands carry numbers; only islands whose number divides by the next sign's rule are solid (e.g. the multiples of 3, then of 4); wrong ones are floating mats; the rule board sits on the third island. · second rule: read the board before the third island; second betrayal: the board changes at island 24. · door: island 48. · length: marathon (3 checkpoints) · closest: roulette PNJ-06; different: a number rule.
9. **Adda (the tea-stall circle)** · hook: evening street life with chai ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: push through · betrayal: a circle of uncles arguing blocks the lane; they part only when Dad stands still for three beats (listening); moving makes them tighten. · second rule: stand still; second betrayal: the third circle opens on a trap if you stand still too long. · door: the stall's back door. · length: "how would I know" · closest: `knock` (taken, unused) and statues HYD-04; different: stillness makes the crowd part.
10. **Rubber Sheet Line (drying sheets)** · hook: hung sheets (CITIES.md) · expect: curtains · betrayal: sheets tacky at the bottom; pass at the top. Weak; closest tar (kit); builders may drop.
11. **Rickshaw Rank** · hook: pedal rickshaws wait in rows ([WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)) · expect: step on a waiting rickshaw · betrayal: the puller pedals off when a customer appears at the stall opposite. Weak; closest autos HYD-04; builders may drop.
12. **Neermahal Sandhya (finale)** · calls back only Agartala ideas: the sticky-then-solid latex (1), the battery rickshaw (2), the unrolled mat (3), the cane weave (4), the pineapple chain (5) and the stair wake (7), crossing the lake to the palace at dusk. · door: the palace door. · length: finale.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky warm cream **#F4E4C1**; hills tropical green **#3E7D4F**; tiles palace white **#EDE7DA**; accent pineapple gold **#E3A712** (unverified).
- Skyline: a white palace with three domes, rubber rows, palm and bamboo clumps, a flat lake horizon.
- Ground cap: white marble on the palace, red earth on the plantation, bamboo slat in the markets.
- Weather: wet-season afternoon cloud, steam off the street.
- Script: Bengali script (Agartala, "আগরতলা", [WV-AGT](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Agartala)); Kokborok is written in Roman and Bengali script (unverified).
- Music: 96 bpm, light, pentatonic with a Bengali-flavoured bansuri lead (unverified) and a bamboo-click rhythm; no real songs.
- Postcard line: "Agartala: a white palace, a lake palace and a pineapple in every basket."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Insurgency, the border, Akhaura and the 1971 war; AFSPA; religious sites and festivals (Tripura Sundari, Chaturdasha, Kharchi, temples, mosque, Buddha temple, Unakoti as anything but scenery); caste and "backward class" remarks about rickshaw pullers (WV-AGT wording must not be used); real sweet shops and hotels; football clubs; reflections and mirrors (taken); monkeys (taken); floods (taken).
