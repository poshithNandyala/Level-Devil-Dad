# SHL · Shillong, Meghalaya: city bible

Status: research draft. Facts carry a link or are marked (unverified). Lines: V = every word checked in a listed source, U = unverified (fallback given). Every concept names its closest used idea. Hook split across the seven NE/Delhi cities (so none repeat): **SHL = rain gear, growing roots, caves, shared taxis, market day**; ITA = sunrise and cane bridges; KOH = hornbill/morung/hill lanes; IMP = floating ground and polo; AIZ = stilt houses, stairs, bamboo poles; AGT = palace water, rubber, rickshaws; DEL = fog, wires, pigeons, roundabouts.

## 1. Identity (3 lines)
- To the people who live there: a cool pine-hill town at about 1,500 m ([W-SHL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shillong)) where Khasi family life (mother is head of the house, youngest daughter inherits, [WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)), church on Sunday, football, guitars and "Bah"/"Kong" manners fit into a small, slow, polite city. Sundays are quiet; shops shut ([WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)).
- Visitors come for: "Scotland of the East" hills, Umiam lake, Elephant Falls, and the day trips: Sohra's falls and caves, Nongriat's double-decker root bridge, Dawki's clear river, Mawlynnong's clean village ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong), [WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)).
- Pride and laughter: the rain is a daily guest, not a story (Mawsynram is the wettest village, [W-CHR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrapunji)); and the joke Shillongites tell about themselves is that the wettest place has a water shortage in winter ([W-CHR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrapunji): "acute water shortage"). Traffic is slow and polite for India ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)).

## 2. Famous places as mechanic seeds (12)
Each: place · what physically happens · generic shape · one betrayal.
1. **Living root bridges, Sohra/Nongriat** · people guide rubber-fig aerial roots across a stream for 10-15 years; the bridge strengthens as it grows; oldest in use >500 years ([W-LRB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_root_bridge), [W-CHR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrapunji)) · a gnarled arch of intertwined brown ropes with leaf fringe · betrayal: the root is only strong where it was guided; a loose hanging root looks the same.
2. **Double-decker bridge (Umshiang), Nongriat** · two living bridges stacked; reached by about 3,500 stone steps down, 3 km; Rainbow Falls one hour further ([MTO Nongriat](https://www.meghalayatourism.in/explore/destinations/by-region/khasi-hills/nongriat/)) · two parallel arches, one above the other · betrayal: the top deck looks like the shortcut; it belongs to a different timer.
3. **Mawsmai / limestone caves, Sohra** · caves with stalactites and stalagmites; Krem Puri is the longest sandstone cave, ~25 km ([WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)) · pale spikes from ceiling and floor facing each other · betrayal: they slowly grow towards each other (see concept 4).
4. **Nohkalikai Falls** · highest plunge fall in India, 1,115 ft ([WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)); the name comes from a sad legend, so use only the falls as a shape, no story · tall thin white ribbon off a cliff edge · betrayal: the cliff edge you walk on has no rail.
5. **Elephant Falls** · stepped waterfall just outside the city ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · three white steps · betrayal: use the steps as a staircase of slick landings (waterfalls are heavily used, so only as dressing).
6. **Umiam lake** · reservoir ~20 km north with water sports ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · long blue ribbon between pine ridges · seed: scenery only (boats and lakes are taken).
7. **Dawki, Umngot river** · 1932 British suspension bridge; the river is so clear that boats look afloat in air ([W-DWK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawki), CITIES.md) · low green steel suspension span with wooden deck · betrayal: sway (concept 11). Border is context only, never a theme.
8. **Mawlynnong** · village famed as Asia's cleanest, bamboo dustbins, 95 households, community clean-up rule ([W-MWL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlynnong)) · bamboo-slat bins on poles, swept lanes · betrayal: everything you drop or leave is swept away (concept 9).
9. **Ïewduh (Iew Duh), Shillong** · largest open street market in the Northeast: fruit, vegetables, honey, fish ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · tarp stalls in rows on a slope · betrayal: stalls swap places (concept 8).
10. **Police Bazaar** · the noisy central crowd, momo and jalebi shops, taxis dropping everyone here ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · a road wedge of shopfronts · betrayal: crowd flow is one-way, a taxi stand is the exit.
11. **Ward's Lake / Lady Hydari Park** · garden lake and park with a small zoo and swings ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · garden bridge over a pond with swings and see-saws · seed: see-saw as a platform (check it is not a repeat before use: balance scale DMN-06 is a weighing; a see-saw is a lever).
12. **Shillong Peak / Mawphlang forest** · highest point of the state (1,965 m), view of the city; sacred groves 25 km away with ferns and orchids ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · pine ridge with a mast on top; mossy trunks · seed: cloud floor at the top; treat the grove as scenery only (it is a sacred site).

## 3. Everyday life hooks
- **Transport.** Black-and-yellow shared taxis, always shared, driver picks up more passengers on the way, fares ₹20-50 inside the city; maroon city buses with a conductor and ticket machine; Sumo/Bolero shared SUVs to Guwahati, 8 to a car; no railway in the state; helicopter to Guwahati ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)). Trap: the taxi leaves only when it is full (concept 3). City traffic is slow and disciplined ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)).
- **Rain gear.** The knup (ka-noop): a bamboo-and-leaf shield from head to below the knees, wide at the shoulders, tapering to a point; no handle, hands free; stays on in wind ([T+L Asia](https://www.travelandleisureasia.com/in/destinations/knup-meghalayas-rain-shield/), [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/no-plastic-or-metal-only-bamboo-and-palm-leaves-meghalayas-100-year-old-umbrellas-do-not-have-a-handle-but-can-protect-you-from-heavy-rains/articleshow/131740476.cms)). Trap: concept 1.
- **Street food and hidden-item candidates (3).** (1) **jadoh**: red rice cooked with pork and turmeric ([cuisinevoila](https://cuisinevoila.com/shillong-street-food)) · (2) **pumaloi** (steamed rice-flour snack; also putharo, pukhlieng, wrapped in banana leaves, [GOYA](https://www.goya.in/blog/the-delicious-world-of-khasi-tea-time-snacks)) · (3) **tungrymbai** (fermented soybean, strong smell; [cercatravel](https://cercatravel.com/shillong-india/)). Recommend **pumaloi** as the hidden item: light, shaped, and not an animal product; jadoh as the end-of-stage "plate" prop. Also momos (steamed, pork/chicken, [WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) and jalebi at Police Bazaar. Trap: a steaming basket whose lid pops (steam is used in TVM-03, so avoid steam as hazard; use leaf-wrap as the shape).
- **Weather.** Heavy monsoon, longest rainy season in India; the city is cleanest in the rain ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)); Sohra holds the record rainfall for a month and a year ([W-CHR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrapunji)); winter water shortage. Trap: concept 5.
- **Animals.** Butterflies (a butterfly museum, [WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)); deer in Lady Hydari Park; the red panda lives in Garo Hills (Nokrek, [WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)). Do not use monkeys.
- **Crafts.** Bamboo and cane handicraft, woollen shawls, bamboo shoot pickle ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)); knup weaving.
- **Sports and games.** Football and guitars are everywhere (CITIES.md, "a big guitar and rock music culture"; football is used by PNJ-03, so only as dressing). Archery/teer: AVOID (betting). Children's games: (unverified) marble games and spinning tops.
- **Festivals (respect rule: only as backdrop, never as a hazard).** Nongkrem (Khasi autumn dance), Shad Suk Mynsiem (spring dance of Khasi women in silver crowns; held in Shillong, unverified date), Wangala (Garo harvest drums). Show costumes as scenery only; no hit, no fall, no joke on the dance. Dance rings are used in GNR-07.
- **Habits and sayings.** Address elders "Bah" (man) and "Kong" (woman); thank "Khublei shibun" ([MTO](https://www.meghalayatourism.in/explore/about-meghalaya/indigenous-communities-&-traditions/)). Sunday quiet. Khasi bakes with little spice and boils or roasts ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)). Shillong speech freely mixes Khasi, English and Hindi ([ISCA](https://www.isca.me/IJSS/Archive/v9/i1/3.ISCA-IRJSS-2019-030.pdf)).

## 5. Twelve stage concepts (builder picks 9 + the tour)
Tour (01) betrayal idea: walking tour where Tara stops at "view point" with a rain umbrella; the first rain drop is a fake: only if you stop under the cloud shape does the real one hit.

1. **Knup (the rain shield)** · hook: the bamboo rain shield, hands-free · expect: shield = rain proof · betrayal: you put it on at a stall; it makes Dad wide (hitbox as wide as the shield, one-way upward) so low doorways and branch gaps are too narrow: you must take it off before, but the rain that waits after the gap is heavy. · second attempt: take it off at the arch, run the rain under the next awning. · second betrayal: without it the awning's drip is a spike row; with it too wide. · door: a doorway that is half-wide; knup off, door is fine. · length: standard · closest: tiny Dad (a smaller body) and tarp TVM-01; different: the body is wider and the gear is optional per gap.
2. **Root Guide (growing the bridge)** · hook: guided rubber-fig roots, [W-LRB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_root_bridge) · expect: roots will grow to meet you · betrayal: you touch one of three bamboo guides at the gap's edge; that root grows (visibly, ~6 s) into a bridge; the other two wither; the shortest looks right but the guided root grows to the bank where rain is coming. · second rule: after it grows, stand on it and a second bridge sprouts across the next gap (waiting works only on roots you already guided). · second betrayal: a late root grows into your head's path. · door: a door only the grown bridge reaches. · length: long · closest: painted path DMN-07 and rice into hills RPR-04 (you build while you go); different: you choose the line first, wait, and the choice is permanent.
3. **Full Sumo (taxi leaves when full)** · hook: shared taxis, 8 to a car, [WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong) · expect: hop in and go · betrayal: the taxi will not leave with Dad alone; three waiting passengers stand on platforms that move only when you walk to them; each follows you in a line and weighs a spring-plate down; bring them back before the horn (taxi pulls away when full or at the timer). · second rule: the third passenger will not follow if Dad ran (only if he walked). · second betrayal: the fourth person is Tara, and the car is now full: Dad must stand on the roof rack. · door: the car door slides to the rack. · length: long · closest: a plank passed hand to hand DMN-04 and packed train BOM-03; different: followers that must be gathered then delivered, the car leaves on a count.
4. **Mawsmai (the growing cave)** · hook: stalactite and stalagmite pairs ([WV-MEG](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Meghalaya)) · expect: a dark cave tour · betrayal: each drip makes its pair grow one step closer (dripping is audible); a pair that has met is a wall; a pair that hasn't is a gap; pass pairs while open; pace follows a drip rhythm you hear, not a clock. · second rule: a tapped stalactite stops growing for a drip. · second betrayal: the stop only works on the one your head hit, not on the pair you passed. · door: a column that has just closed behind. · length: marathon (cave, 3 checkpoints) · closest: crushing rising walls (kit) and ceiling drops; different: growth is caused by your own steps via drips and stops at a tap.
5. **Wettest Desert (Sohra's water)** · hook: record rain with winter water shortage, [W-CHR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherrapunji) · expect: water everywhere, so water is safe · betrayal: a waterfall of paint-like water you may not touch; the one gutter pipe that fills a tank is dry until it rains for exactly the second you're not under it; you must be in the dry, which is why you stand beside the fall. · second rule: the rain is a cloud that follows the tank; stand out of its shadow. · second betrayal: a second cloud, bigger, over the tank. · door: the tank's tap as the exit (tank full opens). · length: how would I know (the rule "the tank fills only when you aren't under the rain" has no sign) · closest: tarp TVM-01 and the storm cloud RNC-04; different: the cloud is a filling meter, not a lightning rod.
6. **Eight-Day Market (Ïewduh)** · hook: largest open market of the NE ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)); traditional Khasi markets keep an eight-day cycle (unverified) · expect: stalls are fixed · betrayal: the stalls rearrange between 8 rows; each time you reach a checkpoint the "day" advances; the gap in row N is where row N-1 had a stall. · second rule: read the stall sign colour: today's colour is shown on the first tarp. · second betrayal: the sign is changed at the last row. · door: the stall that has just sold out. · length: marathon (8 sections, one per day, 3 checkpoints) · closest: roulette floors PNJ-06 and shuffled boxes HYD-07; different: a fixed-day cycle driven by your progress, no randomness.
7. **Pine Peak Cloud Floor (Shillong Peak)** · hook: the 1,965 m peak, view, radar ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)) · expect: a clear viewpoint · betrayal: a cloud bank drifts across the viewpoint platform; the drifting bank is solid for exactly the tiles it covers while lit by sun; a gap appears when it thins. · second rule: clouds thin at the edge, not the middle. · second betrayal: the edge is a drop. · door: a viewpoint rail door. · length: standard · closest: mist clouds (kit) and sunbeams GNR-03; different: the cloud is a platform that thins.
8. **Sway (the Dawki bridge)** · hook: 1932 suspension span ([W-DWK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawki)) · expect: a steady bridge · betrayal: the deck swings as you walk; walking at the swing's rhythm damps it, running amplifies it; a gust of boats' wake adds an extra swing at the third span. · second rule: stop to let it settle; a stop at the tower tilts the deck. · second betrayal: two people on the bridge (an NPC walks the other way) double the swing. · door: the far tower. · length: standard · closest: swaying pot tower JAI-03 and sagging ropes PNJ-05; different: the span's swing is your own step cadence made visible.
9. **Mawlynnong Sweep (the clean village)** · hook: swept lanes and bamboo bins ([W-MWL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlynnong)) · expect: a clean peaceful village · betrayal: the village sweeper brushes each lane in one direction: anything on the lane (including Dad) is pushed; leaving one step on the bin pad opens a bin that holds the door key. · second rule: step on a bin pad and the brush skips that lane for one pass. · second betrayal: the next lane's bin eats the key and gives a leaf. · door: the village gate when the lanes are spotless. · length: standard · closest: treadmills/moving walkways BOM-02; different: pushes are timed sweeps with a safe pad, tied to bins.
10. **Seven Sisters Split (Sohra's parallel falls)** · hook: seven-stream falls near Sohra (unverified; widely listed) · expect: pick any stream · betrayal: only one of seven channels is a safe drop; each lights up as water rises, and your first choice at the top is the one the water leaves. · second rule: the safe one is the one that ran dry first. · second betrayal: a stream runs dry to lure you, then fills. · door: a pool below the last stream. · length: "how would I know" (the dry stream trap needs one failed drop) · closest: the falls rule RNC-07 (fall damage) and `khoh`; different: the drops are a choice between seven channels with a hidden schedule, no fall damage rule.
11. **Soundcheck (guitar lane)** · hook: Shillong's rock culture (CITIES.md) · expect: loud bass hits like GNR-04 · betrayal: amplifiers on a stage push by volume; the volume knob is on the wall and Tara turns it; you can turn it down at a knob (which also silences the platform that holds you). · second rule: lower the knob only after the pit. · second betrayal: an amp one step ahead is on the wrong dial. · door: a stage curtain. · length: standard · closest: shockwaves GNR-04; different: you control the dial, and lowering it kills a platform.
12. **Down Seven Hundred (marathon trek)** · hook: steps down to Nongriat and the double-decker ([MTO](https://www.meghalayatourism.in/explore/destinations/by-region/khasi-hills/nongriat/)) · expect: a long staircase down · betrayal: stairs come in flights of 100; each flight's landing holds a "rest stall" (jadoh) that is a trap, eating it fills you (a full-stomach jump penalty exists in AMR-01, so use a different effect: the landing tilts); the double-decker's top deck is a decoy. · finale: **calls back only this city's own ideas**: the knup (1), the grown root (2), the cave drip (4), the sweeping brush (9). The last landing is a knup-wide doorway into a root that must be guided first. · door: bottom deck's gate. · length: finale + marathon · closest: Hundru RNC-07 (down then up); different: it is dressed as the finale and has no fall rule, only combined mechanics.

Door endings above are ideas, not ids: grep `endings*.ts` before locking one.

## 6. Look and sound
- Palette: sky pale cool grey-blue **#9FB8C7**; hills deep pine green **#2F5D45**; tiles wet slate/stone **#5C6F66**; accent rain-leaf yellow-green **#C8D86B**; (unverified, chosen from photos and descriptions).
- Skyline: pine ridge; Assam-type slanting-roof houses with many windows and wooden floors ([WV-SHL](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Shillong)); a tower mast on the peak; a church spire (silhouette only).
- Ground cap: moss over wet stone; roots at edges.
- Weather: constant drizzle layer, drifting cloud banks.
- Script: Latin (Khasi is written in Roman letters; [KHB](https://learnkhasihere.blogspot.com/)); the game's local script line: "Shillong".
- Music: slow 76 bpm, minor pentatonic with a bright major lift; rhythm instrument: a light duitara-style plucked string (CITIES.md names it) over soft rain-percussion; no real songs.
- Postcard line: "Shillong: where the rain is a neighbour and everyone says Bah."

## 7. Avoid (hard no)
Teer / archery betting; insider/outsider tensions; the border at Dawki (use only the river); conflict, politics, mining or coal; churches or the glass mosque as hazards; sacred forests and the Khasi memorial stones as hazards; the Nohkalikai legend; festival dancers as obstacles; real bands, choirs, football clubs, people; brands; jokes about food (pork, beef, fermented items) or Sunday observance; wind-gust and see-through-water mechanics (taken).
