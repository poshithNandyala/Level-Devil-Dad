| LKO-01 | Aadaab Lucknow (tour) | LKO-F1 `sold-bolt-hooked-out-sideways` [B3, bolt-unrolls-curtain] | Chowk's cloth bolts hung over a dug-up lane are the way across; land on one and it's sold: the shopkeeper's bamboo hook rises, grips, and slides it out sideways from under you. Then a long bolt unrolls down across the road, a wooden roller on its end: run under before it lands | (tour) | plain door by the Gomti (`lko-gomti-ghat`) |
| LKO-02 | Pehle Aap | `politeness-bow-sets-order` [bow-order] | The first one through the haveli doorway gets the loose threshold stone. Lean into the two gentlemen and they see you through first; stand still (a bow) and one goes first and drops the stone; bow again instead of taking the second one's offer and he sees you through himself | `lko02-gali`: a man behind offers you the way, stand still and he goes first; `lko02-takrar`: a stand-off at a doorway, whoever stops bowing first goes first, keep bowing till he gives up; `lko02-dakiya`: the postman and the milkman ride up behind you, men in a hurry don't bow back: step up on the chabutra and let both by | `lko-kiwad`: the haveli door opens outwards, its leaves sweep the two tiles in front of it |
| LKO-03 | Chhapa | `print-washed-stitch-remains` [wash-remains] | Printed blue vine, solid white stitched flowers and white jali over the dhobi's vat. First whistle washes the print, second the jali: stand only on solid flowers | `lko03-kunda`: three vats where the print is the only way, run it without stopping; `lko03-adda`: the karigar's embroidery hoop carries you over the vat from flower to flower, step off on the far side (waiting on the next flower gets you clamped); `lko03-rang`: the rangrez dips the far half of the muslin into his neel, stitch and all: cross it in one go as it comes up | `lko-balti`: the door is real, but its threshold is cloth over the vat; the print washes and the dhobi's bucket comes down on whoever is on the cloth |
| LKO-04 | Makhan Malai | `sun-direction-sinks-foam` [light-sinks] | Every heap of malai: the tile facing the low sun goes soft and sinks under you; the tile in its own long shadow holds. The halwai lifts his shiny lid and the light comes from the other side | `lko04-chowk`: single cups shaded only by the long shadow of a kulhad stack, the shadows shorten as the sun climbs; `lko04-badal`: a cloud's moving shadow over single cups, go with it, not ahead, not behind | `lko-kulhad`: the halwai stokes his bhatti behind you: light from the left, the left tiles of the steps go soft |
| LKO-05 | Galouti (lives: act) | `flatten-platforms-build-low-lane` [flatten-lane] | A hop off a full heap of galouti puts your head in the seekh rack; stand a moment and it squashes flat and stays; from flat your hop clears. Flat is thin: linger and the tawa burns through | `lko05-tawa`: six heaps, some already thin (hop at once); `lko05-lane`: a slab under the lowest rack squashes from your feet forward; `lko05-ulta`: the cook presses the slab from his end with his kafgir, wait off it; `lko05-bhara`: a low rack he hoists now and then, keep your heap full by stepping between a pair; the last time he only half-hoists it, so squash first; `lko05-dum`: double heaps (two squashes), the last a triple | `lko-sheermal`: the baker slaps a sheermal on the tandoor's inside wall: a ledge until it bakes |
| LKO-06 | Kathak (lives: act) | `sound-draws-dancer-spin` [sound-draws] | Red ghungroo strips in the stage floor ring when stepped on and the dancer spins across to the sound; the board she taps rings too: go over it on the musicians' takht | `lko06-tatkar`: her tatkar patch moves to and fro; hop it as it comes at you; `lko06-mehfil`: she spins the stage end to end without stopping, shelter on the musicians' takhts | `lko-sam`: three chakkars and a stamp; the door opens on the stamp; wait on the takht |
| LKO-07 | Hazratganj (marathon, lives: act) | `pace-sets-crowd-shape` [pace-crowd, ladder-block] | Rush (no pause for half a second) and the strollers close ranks and tut you back into the nala; pause and they part | `lko07-shaam` ten awnings come down behind you at dusk (momentum); `lko07-batti` three lamplighters swing their ladders (shelter, then go under); `lko07-julus` a slow procession going your way: walk behind at its pace, wait at each nala (the last one comes early); `lko07-saamne` groups coming the other way under a banner: shelter in the alcoves, the last alcove's sill is cracked (the shelter is the trap once); `lko07-chauraha` stroll so they part, then run before the awnings over them come down | `lko-darban`: the café's darban holds the door only for strollers; land off the nala hop rushing and he shoves you back |
| LKO-08 | Bhool Bhulaiya (HWIK, lives: act) | `entry-height-routes-level` [enter-height] | Every arch is alike: through one at a jump and you come out a floor up; on foot and you stay. The first has a little drain (a hop takes you up on to the hooks), the second the old well beyond it (jump) | `lko08-chausar` (Pachisi reserve): two half courts with a calm chabutra between; a cowrie throw lights that many squares ahead, only lit ones hold, never the courtier's; `lko08-minar`: three arches stacked a floor apart, jump, jump, walk; `lko08-mehrab`: a low vault (walk) then the well (jump); `lko08-utar`: the hanging arch takes you a floor DOWN if you come through it falling | `lko-489`: the last arch at the end of the gallery: walk through and step off to the door; hop the crack into it and you're on the roof |
| LKO-09 | Kabootarbazi (lives: act) | `flag-colour-steers-flock` [flag-signal] | The flyer's flag: red, the flock circles; white, it comes down on the roof just ahead of you. Cross only early in the red | `lko09-gumbad` (a Chota Imambara-style garden tank): white domes seem bigger the farther you stand, jump from the red paving line; the third is the reflection; `lko09-hawa`: the flyer is behind you and the flag has two faces, the birds go by the far face (death `lko-hawa`); `lko09-jhande`: two flyers, the flock obeys the nearer flag | `lko-chhatri`: the pigeon loft's door: on white the flock comes home through it |
| LKO-10 | Lakhnavi Tehzeeb (finale, lives: act) | `finale-bow-meets-bells` [bow-vs-bells] | The gentlemen at the door again, but the floor before it is bells sewn under chikan covers: a step is silent, a bow (standing still) rings them and the dancer spins to you. Bow on the one plain board | `lko10-dhulai` wash line + malai heaps in the low sun; `lko10-darbar`: the court again, but the courtier bows you on to the square between ('pehle aap') and it does not hold; `lko10-julus`: the procession again over covered bells, pause only on the plain boards; `lko10-galiyara`: arches + covered bells | `lko-tehzeeb`: the last door opens to a bow; bow from the plain board two steps back, not on the covered threshold |
