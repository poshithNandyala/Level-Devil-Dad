# Wave 1 build notes (South India + map)

Working notes so the build can be resumed at any point.

## Decisions
- Journey = all state/UT capitals south→north (`src/game/cities.ts` CHAPTERS, 36 entries).
  Playable now: TVM, KVT (bonus), CHN, PDY, BLR, AMR, HYD, PNJ, BOM, DMN (bonus): 8 stages each (80).
- Language rules live in `src/game/cities.ts` LINES (verified words only; no slurs/religion/politics).
  Dropped from research: Attukal pongala hazards, hartal jokes, ashram/Auroville, stupa slide,
  Amaravati construction-delay jokes, film dialogues, 'baigan mein mil gaya' kept (verified).
- Jump: tap ≈ 2.4 tiles (minJump 0.11 s), full hold ≈ 3.8 tiles, ledge assist 7 px.
  `tests/jumpaudit.test.ts` fails if a required jump needs > 15 ticks hold (unless step `long: true`).
  LEGACY now holds only BOM-02/04/05 (Mumbai preview): tune them in the Maharashtra wave.
- Trap kit: `src/game/entities/traps.ts` (Thing, Roller, Shot, Gust, Spring, Blinker, Arc, Cloud,
  Collectible) + helpers in `src/game/levels/kit.ts`; sprites in `src/render/art/things.ts`.
- World: `w.flipControls(seconds)` swaps left/right; player `pushVx` for wind; `Solid.sticky` = fresh tar
  (walk speed `PHYS.stickyRun`).
- More kit (v0.2): Geyser (bubbles, then a spray column; pulses per period), Rider (cyclist, road roller;
  `facesRight`), Panel (decor back wall), Roller `ghost`/`breakOnWall`, TileGroup `paint`
  ({fill, trim, line, windows?, kerb?}) for yellow walls, buildings, tar, traffic islands.
- New cause lines need entries in `CAUSE_LINES` (props.ts). Older stages took their Tara lines from LINES.
- v0.3 kit: Cascade (downward waterfall stream, `forced`/`surge`), Cable (ropeway line), Blinker `flicker`,
  Geyser `fire` (stunt flames), sprites plank/sub/bangle strings (garland look 'bangles'), runner `steer`/`brace`.
- Bilingual lines: "regional | English" (`src/game/bi.ts`); bubbles show the English ~2 s later.
- Scenes (`src/render/scenes.ts`): famous places around each state, used only inside stages (never map pins).
  The stage banner shows the place name for a moment at the start.
- Transitions: circle wipe opens on Dad and closes on the door (`App.irisOpen/irisClose`), stage banner
  (`App.stageBanner`), compact city postcard on a first visit, ⇄ marker over Dad while controls are flipped.
- Map: `src/map/india-data.ts` generated by `scripts/geo2ts.py` from udit-001/india-maps-data
  (districts dissolved to states with mapshaper at a 2.5 km interval, LCC projection, official boundary,
  Lakshadweep drawn 3x so it shows). City pins in MAP_CITY_XY; label sides in `LABEL_AT` (mapview.ts).

## Level design rules (from the Level Devil study)
- One idea per stage, then a second trap that punishes the obvious fix for the first.
- 3–10 s clear once known; instant retry; same behaviour every attempt.
- Every required jump works with a tap (≤125 ms); full-height jumps only where taught.
- Each stage hides one collectible in a risky/secret spot (optional, for 3rd star).

## Stars
1 = clear, 2 = beat par time, 3 = got the hidden item.

## v0.14.4: GNR-07's door, made clear and fair
- The owner couldn't tell what "It's behind the lamp! Join the dance!" wanted. Measured with a human-timing
  model (a player who read the hint, stops at a sloppy spot, arrives at a random moment): 31% got through at
  average timing. Two causes: you could only join from a 2-pixel spot (closer and the dancers sweeping the end
  hit you; further and jump was just a jump), and nothing said how to join or when to jump again.
- Now: press jump anywhere from about two steps short of the ring's left end up to the end, when no dancer is
  at the end, and Dad is pulled into the dance (a short slide, no snapping). With a dancer at the end it's just
  a jump: no more dying from the join itself. While he's right behind the lamp the door glows: jump then.
- New lines: approaching, "Daabe chhede kudo, raas ma jodao! | Jump at the left end, join the dance!"; at the
  locked door, "Garbo ni pachhal! Daabe thi kudo! | Behind the lamp! Hop in from the left!"; once dancing,
  "Garbo ni pachhal aavo tyare kudo! | Jump when you're behind the lamp!" (each dropped if it's stale).
- Result: 95% / 93% / 92% for quick / average / relaxed timing. The check is now a test anyone can rerun and
  copy: `tests/fair-gnr07.test.ts`.
